# Taittirīya Saṃhitā 3.1.2

## अनुवाकप्रवेशः

### Sāyaṇa

(अथ तृतीयकाण्डे प्रथमप्रपाठके द्वितीयोऽनुवाकः)।\
आद्ये संकीर्तितौ मन्त्रौ वृष्टौ चामेध्यदर्शने।\
\
	द्वितीयानुवाके सोमोपस्थानमन्त्राश्चत्वार उत्पाद्यन्ते-\

### Sāyaṇa Translation

Sāyaṇa notes that the first anuvāka treated the two contingency mantras used when the dīkṣita is rained upon or sees something impure. The second anuvāka now begins by teaching four formulas addressed to Soma before his purchase.

### Notes

#### Structural: transition from 3.1.1

The movement is from protection of the consecrated sacrificer in 3.1.1 to the ritual preparation and establishment of Soma and the officiants in 3.1.2.

## सोमस्य साम्राज्यम् : गायत्री, त्रिष्टुभ्, जगती तथा छन्दोमाः

### Saṃhitā

ए॒ष ते॑ गाय॒त्रो भा॒ग इति॑ मे॒ सोमा॑य ब्रूतात् ।
ए॒ष ते॒ त्रैष्टु॑भो॒ जाग॑तो भा॒ग इति॑ मे॒ सोमा॑य ब्रूतात् ।
छन्दो॒माना॒ꣳ॒ साम्रा᳚ज्यं ग॒च्छेति॑ मे॒ सोमा॑य ब्रूतात् ।
यो वै सोम॒ꣳ॒ राजा॑न॒ꣳ॒ साम्रा᳚ज्यं लो॒कं ग॑मयि॒त्वा क्री॒णाति॒ गच्छ॑ति॒ स्वाना॒ꣳ॒ साम्रा᳚ज्यम् ।
छन्दाꣳ॑सि॒ खलु॒ वै सोम॑स्य॒ राज्ञः॒ साम्रा᳚ज्यो लो॒कः ।
पु॒रस्ता॒थ्सोम॑स्य क्र॒यादे॒वम॒भि म॑न्त्रयेत् ।
साम्रा᳚ज्यमे॒वैनं॑ लो॒कं ग॑मयि॒त्वा क्री॑णाति ।
गच्छ॑ति॒ स्वाना॒ꣳ॒ साम्रा᳚ज्यम् ।

### Mūla Translation

The yajamāna has it said to Soma: “This is your Gāyatra share. This is your Traiṣṭubha and Jāgata share. Go to the sovereignty of the Chandomas.”

One who purchases King Soma after first causing him to reach the sovereign world attains sovereignty among his own people. The metres are indeed the sovereign world of King Soma. Therefore, before Soma is purchased, he should be addressed in this way. Having first caused Soma to reach his sovereign world, he purchases him and thereby reaches sovereignty among his own people.

### Sāyaṇa

कृद्गायेद्द्वितीयां त्रिर्गायेत्तृतीयां दशकृत्वो गायेत्। तृतीये पर्याये प्रथमामेका-\
दशकृत्वो गायेद्द्वितीयां सकृद्गायेत्तृतीयां त्रिर्गायेत्। सेयं चतुश्चत्वारिंशस्तोमाख्यस्य च्छन्दोमस्य विष्टुतिः। अथाष्टाचत्वारिंशस्तोमस्य विष्टुतिरेवमाम्नायते-\
	'षोडशभ्यो हिं करोति स तिसृभिः स द्वादशभिः स एकया। षोडशभ्यो हिं करोति स एकया स तिसृभिः स द्वादशभिः। षोडशभ्यो हिं करोति स द्वादशभिः स एकया स तिसृभिरित्येषा वै प्रतिष्ठिताऽष्टाचत्वारिंशस्य विष्टुतिः ' इति। प्रथमे पर्याय प्रथमां त्रिर्गायेत्। मध्यमां द्वादशकृत्वो गायेत्। उत्तमां सकृद्गायेत्। द्वितीये पर्याये प्रथमां सकृद्गायन्मध्यमां त्रिरुत्तमां द्वादशकृत्वः। तृतीये पर्याये प्रथमां द्वादशकृत्वा द्वितीयां सकृत्तृतीयां त्रिर्गायेत्। सेयमष्टाचत्वारिंशस्तामाख्यस्य च्छन्दोमस्य विष्टुतिरिति। द्वादशाहक्रतोरष्टममहश्चतुर्विंशस्तोप्रसाध्यं नवममहश्चतुश्चत्वारिंशस्तोमसाध्यं दशममहरष्टाचत्वारिंशस्तोमसाध्यम्। तस्माच्छन्दोमानां साम्राज्यं द्वादशाहाद्युत्तरक्रतुषु प्राप्यते।\
	एष ते गायत्र इत्यादींश्चतुरो मन्त्रान्विनियोक्तुं प्रस्तौति-\
	यो व सोभामात। यजमानः सोमदेवतारूपं राजानं साम्राज्यरूपं लोकं प्रापय्य पश्चाद्वल्लीरूपं सीमं क्रीणाति। स स्वानां मध्ये साम्राज्यं गच्छति। गायत्रीत्रिष्टुब्जगती-रूपाणि च्छन्दांसि तैरुपलक्षिताश्छन्दोमाश्च सोमस्य राज्ञः साम्राज्युक्तो लोक इत्युच्यते।\
	अथ चतुरो मन्त्रान्सोमाभिमन्त्रणे विनियुङ्क्ते-\
	पुरस्तदिति। एवमिति एष ते गायत्र इत्यादिभिश्चतुर्भिरित्यर्थः। प्राकरणिका-नुवाकसंबन्धस्तु पुरस्तात्सोमस्य क्रयादेव स्पष्टमवगम्यते। सोमं ते क्रीणामीति मन्त्रेण सोमक्रय आरब्धस्तत्पूर्वम  शुना ते अ  शुरित्यनुवाकस्यावसाने प्रजास्त्वामनुप्राणन्त्वित्येतदूर्ध्वमेष ते गायत्र इत्यादयश्चत्वारा मन्त्रा द्रष्ठव्याः।\
पूर्वोक्तयोरबद्धं गन उन्दतीवंलमित्वेतयोर्मन्त्रयोर्यद्यपीदृशं स्पष्टं वचनं नास्ति तथाऽपि दीक्षितस्य वाद इति वाक्येन प्रकरणप्रसिद्धं दीक्षितमनूद्य वादविधानाद्दीक्षितत्वसिद्धेरूर्ध्वभावित्वं गम्यते। दीक्षितत्वमाकूत्यै प्रयुजेऽग्नये स्वाहेत्यनुवाके संपन्नं तदीयब्राह्मणे दीक्षितत्वावेदनस्याऽऽस्नातत्वात् 'स्वाहा यज्ञं वातादारभ इत्याहायं वाव यः पवते स यज्ञस्तमेव साक्षादारभते मुष्टी करोति वाचं यच्छति यज्ञस्य धृत्या अदीक्षिष्टायं ब्राह्मण इति त्रिरुपा  श्वाह' इति। तस्माद्यज्ञं वातादारभ इत्येतस्मादुपर्यबद्धं मन उन्दतीर्बलमित्येतौ द्रष्टव्यौ। एवमुत्तरत्रापि सर्वत्रौपानुवाक्ये योजनीयम्।\

### Sāyaṇa Translation

Sāyaṇa explains that Soma, visible before the sacrificer in the form of the creeper and about to be purchased, is addressed as King Soma.

The Gāyatra share belongs to the morning pressing and is connected with the Gāyatrī metre. The Traiṣṭubha share belongs to the midday pressing and is connected with Triṣṭubh. The Jāgata share belongs to the third pressing and is connected with Jagatī.

Sāyaṇa then moves from the three metres to the three stomas called the **Chandomas**. They are measured by the syllabic counts associated with Gāyatrī, Triṣṭubh, and Jagatī and are known as the Caturviṃśa, Catuścatvāriṃśa, and Aṣṭācatvāriṃśa stomas. Their special domain is found in extended Soma rites such as the Dvādaśāha. This is why Soma is told to go to the sovereignty of the Chandomas before being purchased.

Sāyaṇa preserves the detailed viṣṭuti patterns for all three stomas.

For the **Caturviṃśa-stoma**, the three rounds are:

`3 + 4 + 1`

`1 + 3 + 4`

`4 + 1 + 3`

giving 24 in all.

For the **Catuścatvāriṃśa-stoma**, the three rounds are:

`3 + 11 + 1 = 15`

`1 + 3 + 10 = 14`

`11 + 1 + 3 = 15`

giving 44 in all.

For the **Aṣṭācatvāriṃśa-stoma**, the three rounds are:

`3 + 12 + 1 = 16`

`1 + 3 + 12 = 16`

`12 + 1 + 3 = 16`

giving 48 in all.

In the Dvādaśāha these characterize the eighth, ninth, and tenth days respectively.

Sāyaṇa then explains the fruit. Soma is first caused to reach his own sovereign domain, the world of metres and stomas. Only afterward is he purchased in creeper form. The yajamāna who performs the sequence in this way attains sovereignty among his own people.

He also locates these four formulas within the broader Aupānuvākya structure: they belong immediately before Soma purchase. This gives a concrete example of the method described in 3.1.1, where unattached material must be connected with its ritual context through appropriate evidence.

### Notes

#### Ritual: Soma's sovereignty

`छन्दोमानां साम्राज्यम्` is not merely a metaphor for political kingship. In Sāyaṇa's interpretation it is the ritual domain constituted by the metres and the Chandoma stomas.

#### Sāman: Caturviṃśa

The Caturviṃśa viṣṭuti distributes the three ṛcs over three rounds as:

`3,4,1 | 1,3,4 | 4,1,3`

#### Sāman: Catuścatvāriṃśa

The Catuścatvāriṃśa pattern is:

`3,11,1 | 1,3,10 | 11,1,3`

#### Sāman: Aṣṭācatvāriṃśa

The Aṣṭācatvāriṃśa pattern is:

`3,12,1 | 1,3,12 | 12,1,3`

#### Cross-reference: Dvādaśāha

Sāyaṇa associates the three stomas with the eighth, ninth, and tenth days of the Dvādaśāha.

#### Structural: Aupānuvākya in practice

This section demonstrates the interpretive method explained at the opening of Kāṇḍa 3. The mantras are not treated in isolation. Their place is established from the fact that the text explicitly says they are recited before Soma purchase.

## तानूनप्त्रस्य प्रतिष्ठा : प्रजापतौ मनसि

### Saṃhitā

यो वै ता॑नून॒प्त्रस्य॑ प्रति॒ष्ठां वेद॒ प्रत्ये॒व ति॑ष्ठति ।
ब्रह्मवा॒दिनो॑ वदन्ति॒ न प्रा॒श्नन्ति॒ न जु॑ह्व॒त्यथ॒ क्व॑ तानून॒प्त्रं प्रति॑ तिष्ठ॒तीति॑ ।
प्र॒जाप॑तौ॒ मन॒सीति॑ ब्रूयात् ।
त्रिरव॑ जिघ्रेत् ।
प्र॒जाप॑तौ त्वा॒ मन॑सि जुहो॒मीत्ये॒षा वै ता॑नून॒प्त्रस्य॑ प्रति॒ष्ठा ।
य ए॒वं वेद॒ प्रत्ये॒व ति॑ष्ठति ।

### Mūla Translation

One who knows the establishment of the Tānūnaptra stands firmly established.

The Brahmavādins ask: “It is neither eaten nor offered. Where, then, is the Tānūnaptra established?”

He should answer: “In Prajāpati, in the mind.”

He should smell it three times, thinking: “I offer you mentally in Prajāpati.”

This is the establishment of the Tānūnaptra. One who knows thus stands firmly established.

### Sāyaṇa

अथ तानूनप्त्रस्यावघ्राणं विधातुं प्रस्तौति-\
	यो वै तानूनेति। तानूनप्त्रस्य त्वा गृह्णामीत्यादिभिर्मन्त्रैश्चमसे यदाज्यं गृह्यते तत्तानूनप्त्रं तस्य प्रतिष्ठा न ज्ञायते। सोमरसस्य तु वह्नै हुतत्वादृत्विग्भिः पतित्वाच्चास्ति प्रतिष्ठितत्वम्। तानूनष्वे तु तदुभयं नास्तीति ग्रह्मवादिभिः पृष्ठो बुद्धिमानुत्तरं ब्रूयान्मनसा प्रजापतौ स्थापनादस्ति प्रतिष्ठेति। \
	इदानीं मन्त्रेणावप्राणं विधत्ते-\
	त्रिरव जिघ्रेदिति। प्रजापतावित्यादिर्मन्त्रः। हे तानूनप्त्र त्वां प्रजापतौ जुहोमीत्येवं मनसि स्मरामीति शेषः। अयं च मन्त्रोऽग्नेरातिथ्यमसीत्यनुवाकस्यान्ते द्रष्टव्यः त एते पूर्वोक्ता मन्त्रा विधयश्व याजमानाः। इत ऊर्धं त्वाध्वर्यवाः। तत्राऽऽश्रावणं विधत्ते-\

### Sāyaṇa Translation

Sāyaṇa explains the ritual problem clearly. The Tānūnaptra is the ghee taken into a camasa with the formulas beginning `तानूनप्त्रस्य त्वा गृह्णामि`.

Ordinary Soma obtains an obvious ritual establishment because it is offered into the fire and consumed by the priests. Tānūnaptra, however, is neither offered nor eaten. Its pratiṣṭhā is therefore not immediately evident.

The Brahmavādin question asks where such a substance can be said to stand established.

The answer is mental. The Tānūnaptra is placed in Prajāpati in the mind:

`प्रजापतौ त्वा मनसि जुहोमि`

“I offer you mentally in Prajāpati.”

The threefold smelling is the prescribed ritual action accompanying this mental offering.

Sāyaṇa also specifies the ritual placement of the mantra, saying that it is to be understood at the end of the relevant earlier anuvāka. He then marks a transition: the material up to this point belongs to the yajamāna, while what follows concerns the Adhvaryu.

### Notes

#### Ritual: why mental pratiṣṭhā is necessary

The problem arises precisely because the Tānūnaptra lacks the two ordinary modes by which a sacrificial substance becomes visibly integrated into the rite:

it is not offered,

and it is not consumed.

#### Study note: mental offering

The phrase:

`प्रजापतौ त्वा मनसि जुहोमि`

is not a figurative gloss added by us. It is the explicit ritual solution given by the text and explained by Sāyaṇa.

#### Structural: yajamāna to Adhvaryu

Sāyaṇa explicitly marks the transition from yajamāna material to Adhvaryu material at this point.

## अध्वर्योः प्रतिष्ठा : आश्रावणात् होमपर्यन्तम्

### Saṃhitā

यो वा अ॑ध्व॒र्योः प्र॑ति॒ष्ठां वेद॒ प्रत्ये॒व ति॑ष्ठति ।
यतो॒ मन्ये॒तान॑भिक्रम्य होष्या॒मीति॒ तत्तिष्ठ॒न्ना श्रा॑वयेत् ।
ए॒षा वा अ॑ध्व॒र्योः प्र॑ति॒ष्ठा ।
य ए॒वं वेद॒ प्रत्ये॒व ति॑ष्ठति ।
यद॑भि॒क्रम्य॑ जुहु॒यात्प्र॑ति॒ष्ठाया॑ इया॒त् ।
तस्मा᳚थ्समा॒नत्र॒ तिष्ठ॑ता होत॒व्यं॑ प्रति॑ष्ठित्यै ।

### Mūla Translation

One who knows the Adhvaryu's establishment stands firmly established.

From the place where he thinks, “Without stepping forward from here I shall make the offering,” he should give the āśrāvaṇa while standing there. This is the Adhvaryu's establishment.

If he were to step forward and then make the offering, he would depart from his establishment. Therefore he should make the offering while remaining in the same place, for the sake of establishment.

### Sāyaṇa

यो वा अध्वर्योरिति। आश्रावणमारभ्य होमपर्यन्तमेकत्रावस्थानमध्वर्योः प्रतिष्ठा। आहवनीयसमीषादिषु होमदेशेषु मध्ये यस्माद्देशादभिक्रमणमकृत्वा यं होमं होष्यामीति मन्येत तद्धोमार्थं तस्मिन्नेव देशे तिष्ठन्नाश्रावयेत्।\
	आश्रावणदेशादन्यत्र गत्वा होमं निन्दति-\
	यदभिक्रम्येति। इयात्प्रच्यवेत। समानत्र समानदेशे यत्राऽऽश्रावणं तत्रवेत्यर्थः। सोऽयं विधिः सर्वाश्रावणसाधारणत्वान्सर्वत्रान्वेति। प्रयाजेषु त्वभिक्रामं जुहोतीति वाचनिकमभिक्रमणम्।

### Sāyaṇa Translation

Sāyaṇa defines the Adhvaryu's pratiṣṭhā as remaining in one place from the āśrāvaṇa through the offering.

At the point from which the Adhvaryu intends to perform the offering without further stepping forward, he should give the āśrāvaṇa while standing there. The same ritual position is then maintained until the offering itself.

To move to another place after giving the call would mean departing from one's pratiṣṭhā. Hence:

`समानत्र तिष्ठता होतव्यम्`

the offering is to be made while standing in the same place.

Sāyaṇa notes that this is a general rule for āśrāvaṇa. Where a specific rite, such as a Prayāja, explicitly prescribes movement, that explicit prescription governs the exception.

### Notes

#### Ritual: pratiṣṭhā is spatial

Here *pratiṣṭhā* is not abstract stability. It is embodied in the continuity of ritual position from āśrāvaṇa to offering.

#### Rule

`समानत्र तिष्ठता होतव्यम्`

The sacrificer-officiant should offer while remaining in the same ritual place from which the call was made.

#### Mīmāṃsā: explicit exception

A specific textual prescription for movement overrides the general principle. Sāyaṇa cites the Prayāja case as an example.

## अध्वर्योः स्वम् : स्रुक्, वायव्यपात्रम्, चमसः

### Saṃhitā

यो वा अ॑ध्व॒र्योः स्वं वेद॒ स्ववा॑ने॒व भ॑वति ।
स्रुग्वा अ॑स्य॒ स्वम् ।
वा॑य॒व्य॑मस्य॒ स्वम् ।
च॑म॒सो᳚ऽस्य॒ स्वम् ।
यद्वा॑य॒व्यं॑ वा चम॒सं वान॑न्वारभ्याश्रा॒वये॒त् स्वादि॑या॒त् ।
तस्मा॑दन्वा॒रभ्या॒श्राव्य॒ग्ग्॒ स्वादे॒व नैति ।

### Mūla Translation

One who knows what belongs to the Adhvaryu becomes possessed of what is his own.

The ladle is his own. The Vāyavya vessel is his own. The camasa is his own.

If he were to give the āśrāvaṇa without taking hold of either the Vāyavya vessel or the camasa, he would depart from what is his own. Therefore he gives the āśrāvaṇa while holding one of them, and thus he does not depart from what is his own.

### Sāyaṇa

आश्रावणकाले होमसाधनस्य हस्ते धारणं विधत्ते-\
	यो वा अध्वर्योरिति। स्रुग्जुह्वाद्यैन्द्रवायवादिसोमरसग्रहणाधारभूतं पात्रं वायव्यं वायुना तस्य वृतत्वात्, मद्देवत्यान्येव वः पात्राण्युच्यन्ता इति। त स्मान्नानादेवत्यानि सन्ति वायव्यान्युच्यन्त इत्याम्नातः सोमरसोन्नयनपात्रविशेषश्चमसः। यद्यपि स्रुगप्यत्रोपन्यस्ता तथाऽपि सोमब्राह्मणशेषत्वाद्वायव्यचमसयोरेवान्वारम्भणं धारणमत्र विधीयते तेन स्रुचां धारणमप्युलक्ष्यत इति द्रष्टव्यम्। अन्वारभ्य हस्ते गृहीत्वेत्यर्थः।\

### Sāyaṇa Translation

Sāyaṇa explains the Adhvaryu's `स्वम्`, his own ritual possession, through the implements specifically associated with his function.

The list includes the sruj or offering ladle, the Vāyavya vessel used as a Soma receptacle, and the camasa.

Because the immediate context belongs to the Soma ritual, the Vāyavya vessel and camasa are the primary implements relevant to this instruction. The mention of the sruj also indicates the wider principle concerning the Adhvaryu's implements.

`अन्वारभ्य` here means taking hold of the object in the hand. Thus at the time of the āśrāvaṇa he should be physically connected with the ritual implement that is his own. In this way he does not symbolically depart from his own ritual domain.

### Notes

#### Lexical: स्वम्

`स्वम्` here means what properly belongs to the Adhvaryu within the ritual system, especially the implements defining his function.

#### Ritual: अन्वारभ्य

Sāyaṇa glosses the action concretely as taking hold of the implement in the hand.

#### Structural: continuity of the Adhvaryu

The previous unit establishes continuity of **place**.

This unit establishes continuity with the Adhvaryu's **own ritual implement**.

The two ideas belong together.

## सोमस्य प्रतिष्ठा तथा स्तोत्रोपाकरणम्

### Saṃhitā

यो वै सोम॒मप्र॑तिष्ठाप्य स्तो॒त्रमु॑पाक॒रोत्यप्र॑तिष्ठितः॒ सोमो॒ भव॑ति ।
अप्र॑तिष्ठितः॒ स्तोमः ।
अप्र॑तिष्ठितान्यु॒क्थानि ।
अप्र॑तिष्ठितो॒ यज॑मानः ।
अप्र॑तिष्ठितोऽध्व॒र्युः ।
वा॑य॒व्यं॑ वै सोम॑स्य प्रति॒ष्ठा ।
च॑म॒सो᳚ऽस्य प्रति॒ष्ठा ।
सोमः॒ स्तोम॑स्य ।
स्तोम॑ उ॒क्थानाम् ।
ग्रहं॑ वा गृही॒त्वा च॑म॒सं वो॒न्नीय॑ स्तो॒त्रमु॒पाकु॑र्यात् ।
प्रत्ये॒व सोमग्ग्॑ स्था॒पय॑ति ।
प्रति॒ स्तोमम् ।
प्रत्यु॒क्थानि ।
प्रति॒ यज॑मान॒स्तिष्ठ॑ति ।
प्रत्य॑ध्व॒र्युः ॥ ३। १। २॥

### Mūla Translation

If one were to initiate the stotra without first establishing Soma, Soma would be unestablished, the stoma unestablished, the ukthas unestablished, the yajamāna unestablished, and the Adhvaryu unestablished.

The Vāyavya vessel is Soma's establishment, and the camasa is his establishment. Soma is the establishment of the stoma, and the stoma is the establishment of the ukthas.

Therefore one should initiate the stotra only after taking a graha or filling a camasa.

Thereby he establishes Soma. He establishes the stoma. He establishes the ukthas. The yajamāna stands established, and the Adhvaryu stands established.

### Sāyaṇa

अथ प्रातःसवनादिस्तोत्राणां कालविशिष्टमुपाकरणं विधत्ते-\
\
	यो वै सोममिति। स्तोत्रस्योपाकरणप्रकारमापस्तम्बो दर्शेयति- वायुर्हिंकर्तेति प्रस्तोत्रे बर्हिर्मुष्टिं प्रयच्छति सर्वेषु पवमानेष्ववेमुपाकरणोऽसर्ज्यसर्जि वागसर्ज्यैन्द्र  सहोऽसर्ज्युपावर्तध्वमिति बर्हिर्भ्यामन्यानि पवमानेभ्यः स्तोत्राण्युपाकरोति इति। ग्रहे चमसे वा सोमरसस्य प्रक्षेपः प्रतिष्ठापनम्। तदकरणे सोमोऽप्रतिष्ठितो भवति। सोमस्याप्रतिष्ठायां स्तोमस्य प्रगीतमन्त्रसाध्याया (?) स्तुतेनाप्रतिष्ठा भवति। तदप्रतिष्ठायां स्तुतमनुशंसतीति विहितानामप्रगतिमन्त्रसाध्यस्तुतिरूपाणामुक्थशब्दाभिधेयानां शस्त्राणामप्रतिष्ठा भवति। तदप्रतिष्ठायां च यज्ञस्यासिद्धेर्यजमानोऽ–ध्वर्युश्चेत्युभावप्रतिष्ठितौ भवतः। कथं तर्ह्युक्तपरम्पराया उत्तरोत्तरप्रतिष्ठा भवतीति। तदुच्यते-यदि वायव्ये चमसे वा सोमः प्रतिष्ठितो भवति ततः स्तोत्रशस्त्रयजमानाध्वर्यू-णामुत्तरोत्तरप्रतिष्ठा। तस्माद्वायव्य ऊर्ध्वपात्रे सोमरसं गृहीत्वा वा चमसं सोमरसेन पूरयित्वा वा सूणोक्तप्रकारेण स्तोत्रमुपाकुर्यांत्। आश्विनग्रहात्पूर्वभाविष्वैन्द्रवायवादि-ग्रहेषु गृहीतेषु बहिष्पवमानस्य यदुपाकरणं तत्र चमसोन्नयनाभावाद्ग्नहणमेवोपा-करणकालस्योपलक्षणम्। तृतीयसवने पात्नीवतान्तेषु ग्रहेषु हुतेषु चमसान्पूरयित्वा यज्ञायज्ञियस्तोत्रमुपाकरोति। तत्र ग्रहग्रहणाभावाच्चमसोन्नयनमेवोपाकरणकालोपल-क्षणम्। अत एवाऽऽपस्तम्ब आह- होतृचमसमुख्यांश्चमसानुन्नयन्त  राजानमुन्नीय दशामिः कलशौ मृष्ट्वा न्युब्जति यज्ञायज्ञियस्य स्तोत्रमुपाकरोति इति। यदि तृतीयप्र-पाठकेएतद्वै सर्वमध्वर्युरुपाकुर्वन्नुद्गातृभ्य उपाकरोतीत्येष उपाकरणविधिः स्यात्तर्हि तदनुवादेनात्र कालविधिरस्तु। ग्रहचमसोन्नयनोर्ध्वकालभाविना स्तोत्रोपाकरणेन च सोमस्तोमादिपरम्परायाः प्रतिष्ठा संपद्यते। सोमं प्रतिष्ठापयतीत्यन्वयः स्तोत्र मुक्थानीत्यत्रापि तथाऽन्वेतव्यम्।\
\

इति श्रीमत्सायणाचार्यविरचिते माधवीये वेदार्थप्रकाशे कृष्णयजुर्वेदीयतैत्तिरीयसंहिताभाष्ये तृतीयकाण्डे प्रथमप्रपाठके\
द्वितीयोऽनुवाकः॥२॥

### Sāyaṇa Translation

Sāyaṇa now explains the required timing of the stotra-upākaraṇa.

Āpastamba describes the procedure by which the Adhvaryu gives a handful of barhis to the Prastotṛ and thereby authorizes the singing. The present passage is concerned not merely with the procedure itself but with **when** that authorization may properly occur.

Soma must first be ritually established in a graha or camasa. If the stotra were initiated before this, Soma would remain unestablished. Since the stoma depends upon Soma, the stoma would then be unestablished. Since the ukthas depend upon the stoma, they too would be unestablished. The failure would extend through the sacrifice to the yajamāna and the Adhvaryu.

The positive sequence is therefore:

`ग्रहः अथवा चमसः → सोमः → स्तोमः → उक्थानि → यजमानः तथा अध्वर्युः`

A graha is first taken or a camasa filled. Soma is thereby established. Soma establishes the stoma. The stoma establishes the ukthas. Through this ordered chain the yajamāna and Adhvaryu themselves stand established.

Sāyaṇa also clarifies that in different savanas the concrete indicator of the correct time may differ. Where a graha is taken before the stotra, graha-taking marks the required prior establishment. Where the rite instead involves filling the camasas before the relevant stotra, the camasa-filling marks the required time.

Thus the deeper point is not a rigid dependence on one vessel type. The stotra must begin only **after Soma has been ritually established in the appropriate vessel for that context**.

### Notes

#### Ritual: the chain of pratiṣṭhā

The central sequence is:

**graha or camasa → Soma → stoma → uktha → yajamāna and Adhvaryu**

#### Study note: why this matters

The stotra is not an isolated musical act. It depends upon an already established Soma. The ritual sequence therefore binds vessel, Soma, chant structure, śastra, sacrificer, and officiant into one chain.

#### Cross-reference: TS 3.3.2

The actual stotra-upākaraṇa mantra beginning `वायुर्हिंकर्ता` is treated in TS 3.3.2. Here the concern is primarily the **timing and ritual prerequisite** for initiating that stotra.

#### Structural: one idea throughout the second half

From the Tānūnaptra onward, the repeated theme is **pratiṣṭhā**:

Tānūnaptra is established mentally in Prajāpati.

The Adhvaryu is established by continuity of place.

The Adhvaryu remains connected with his own implements.

Soma is established in the vessel.

Soma then establishes the stoma and the rest of the ritual chain.
