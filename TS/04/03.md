# Taittirīya Saṃhitā 3.4.3

## अनुवाकप्रवेशः

### Sāyaṇa

मन्त्राः काम्यपशोरत्र द्वितीये समुदीरिताः ।
अथ तृतीये तद्विधिरुच्यते ।

### Sāyaṇa Translation

The second anuvāka gave the mantras for the kāmya animal rite. The third explains the procedure and the deity-options.

## वशायाः उत्पत्तिकथा

### Saṃhitā

इ॒मे वै स॒हास्तां॒ ते वा॒युर्व्य॑वा॒त्ते गर्भ॑मदधातां॒ तꣳ सोमः॒ प्राज॑नयद॒ग्निर॑ग्रसत ।
स ए॒तं प्र॒जाप॑तिराग्ने॒यम॒ष्टाक॑पालमपश्य॒त्तं निर॑वप॒त्तेनै॒वैना॑म॒ग्नेरधि॒ निर॑क्रीणात् ।

### Mūla Translation

These two, Heaven and Earth, were once together. Vāyu separated them; they conceived a fetus. Soma brought it forth, and Agni swallowed it. Prajāpati perceived an eight-cake offering to Agni, offered it, and by it purchased her back from Agni.

### Sāyaṇa

द्यावापृथिव्यौ पुरा सहाऽऽस्ताम् । वायुर्व्यवात्, वियुक्ते अकरोत् । पुनर्वायुना संपरिष्वक्ते सत्यौ वशारूपं गर्भं धारितवत्यौ । तं सोमोऽजनयत् । अग्निर्ग्रसितवान् । प्रजापतिराग्नेयमष्टाकपालं निरुप्य तामग्नेर्निष्क्रीतवान् ।

### Sāyaṇa Translation

Heaven and Earth were originally joined. Vāyu separated them and then enabled conception. Soma generated the vaśā-fetus, Agni consumed it, and Prajāpati recovered the vaśā from Agni by means of an eight-cake Agneya puroḍāśa.

## आग्नेयमष्टाकपालम्

### Saṃhitā

तस्मा॒दप्य॑न्यदेव॒त्या॑मा॒लभ॑मान आग्ने॒यम॒ष्टाक॑पालं पु॒रस्ता॒न्निर्व॑पेत् ।
अ॒ग्नेरे॒वैना॒मधि॑ नि॒ष्क्रीयाल॑भते ॥

### Mūla Translation

Therefore, even when taking her for another deity, one should first offer an eight-cake puroḍāśa to Agni. Having purchased her from Agni, one then takes her.

### Sāyaṇa Translation

Even when the animal is taken for another deity, the preliminary Agneya offering serves as the purchase-price by which she is first redeemed from Agni.

## सर्वदेवत्या वशा

### Saṃhitā

यद्वा॒युर्व्यवा॒त्तस्मा᳚द्वाय॒व्या॑ ।
यदि॒मे गर्भ॒मद॑धातां॒ तस्मा᳚द्द्यावापृथि॒व्या॑ ।
यथ्सोमः॒ प्राज॑नयद॒ग्निरग्र॑सत॒ तस्मा॑दग्नीषो॒मीया᳚ ।
यद॒नयो᳚र्विय॒त्योर्वागव॑द॒त्तस्मा᳚थ्सारस्व॒ती ।
यत्प्र॒जाप॑तिर॒ग्नेरधि॑ नि॒रक्री॑णा॒त्तस्मा᳚त्प्राजाप॒त्या ।
सा वा ए॒षा स॑र्वदेव॒त्या॑ यद॒जा व॒शा ॥

### Mūla Translation

Because Vāyu separated them, she is Vāyavya. Because Heaven and Earth conceived her, she is Dyāvāpṛthivīya. Because Soma generated her and Agni swallowed her, she is Agnīṣomīya. Because speech arose when the two were separated, she is Sārasvatī. Because Prajāpati purchased her from Agni, she is Prājāpatya. Thus the vaśā-goat is connected with all the gods.

### Sāyaṇa

संश्लिष्टे द्यावापृथिव्यौ यदा वियुज्येते तदा वेणुदलविभागध्वनिः समुत्पन्नस्तदिदं वाचो वदनम् । तस्मादुपकारित्वात्सरस्वत्यत्र देवता ।

### Sāyaṇa Translation

The vaśā is connected with Vāyu, Heaven and Earth, Agni and Soma, Sarasvatī, and Prajāpati. Sāyaṇa gives a striking explanation for Sarasvatī: when Heaven and Earth are separated, a splitting sound arises like bamboo being pulled apart. That sound is treated as the manifestation of speech.

## पञ्च काम्यदेवताः

### Saṃhitā

वा॒य॒व्या॑मा ल॑भेत॒ भूति॑कामः ।
द्यावापृथि॒व्या॑मा ल॑भेत कृ॒षमा॑णः प्रति॒ष्ठाका॑मः ।
अग्नीषो॒मीया॒मा ल॑भेत॒ यः का॒मये॒तान्न॑वानन्ना॒दः स्या॒मिति ।
सारस्व॒तीमा ल॑भेत॒ य ई᳚श्व॒रो वा॒चो वदि॑तोः॒ सन्वाचं॒ न वदे᳚त् ।
प्राजाप॒त्यामा ल॑भेत॒ यः का॒मये॒तान॑भिजितम॒भि ज॑येय॒मिति ॥

### Mūla Translation

One desiring prosperity should take her for Vāyu. A cultivator desiring stable establishment should take her for Heaven and Earth. One desiring to possess food and be capable of eating it should take her for Agni and Soma. One who is capable of speech but cannot speak should take her for Sarasvatī. One desiring to conquer what has not been conquered should take her for Prajāpati.

### Sāyaṇa Translation

The same animal is assigned differently according to the desired result:

Vāyu for prosperity.

Heaven and Earth for agricultural stability.

Agni and Soma for food and the capacity to consume it.

Sarasvatī for one who has the ability to speak but is obstructed from doing so.

Prajāpati for conquering what has not yet been conquered.

### Notes

#### Sāyaṇa on annādya

अन्नाद्यम् = अन्नभक्षणसामर्थ्यम्.

The boon is not merely possession of food but the bodily capacity to make use of it.

## किक्किटाकारः

### Saṃhitā

किक्किटा॒कारं॑ जुहोति ।
किक्किटाका॒रेण॒ वै ग्रा॒म्याः प॒शवो॑ रमन्ते॒ प्रार॒ण्याः प॑तन्ति ॥

### Mūla Translation

He offers with the kikkitā sound. Domestic animals delight in the kikkitā sound, while wild animals flee.

### Sāyaṇa Translation

Domestic animals respond to the kikkitā sound while wild animals flee from it. The sound therefore marks and ritually stabilizes the domestic-animal sphere.

## यजमानस्य मुख्यः कामः

### Saṃhitā

स॒त्याः स॑न्तु॒ यज॑मानस्य॒ कामाः᳚ ॥

### Mūla Translation

May the desires of the yajamāna become true.

### Sāyaṇa

अयं यज्ञोऽनार्तो विघ्नरहित उदृचं समाप्तिं गच्छतीति यदेष एव यजमानस्य मुख्यः कामः ।

### Sāyaṇa Translation

The yajamāna's most fundamental desire is that the sacrifice reach its final verse and completion without mishap.

## उल्बणम्

### Sāyaṇa

विधिमतिक्रम्यानुष्ठितमङ्गमुल्बणम् । अनुल्बणशब्दोच्चारणेन तस्य शान्तिर्भवति ।

### Sāyaṇa Translation

Ulbaṇa is a ritual component performed in violation of the prescribed rule. The utterance anulbaṇa pacifies that procedural fault.

## अधिकारिणः

### Saṃhitā

सा वा ए॒षा त्र॑या॒णामे॒वाव॑रुद्धा संवत्सर॒सदः॑ सहस्रया॒जिनो॑ गृहमे॒धिनः॑ ॥

### Mūla Translation

This vaśā is restricted to three: one engaged in a year-long sattra, one who has performed a thousand-gift sacrifice, and a gṛhamedhin.

### Sāyaṇa Translation

The rite is restricted to three qualified sacrificers: one engaged in a year-long sattra, a sahasrayājin, and a gṛhamedhin.
