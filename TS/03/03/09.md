# Taittirīya Saṃhitā 3.3.9

## अनुवाकप्रवेशः

### Saṃhitā

—

### Sāyaṇa

अष्टमे वेदिदाहोऽयं प्राधान्येन प्रपञ्चितः ।
अथ नवमे वृषालम्भाख्यं किंचित्कर्माभिधीयते ।

### Sāyaṇa Translation

The eighth anuvāka dealt primarily with the burning of the vedi. The ninth now describes a rite connected with the taking of an aged bull.

## एतं युवानम्

### Saṃhitā

ए॒तं युवा॑नं॒ परि॑ वो ददामि॒ तेन॒ क्रीड॑न्तीश्चरत प्रि॒येण॑ ।
मा नः॑ शाप्त ज॒नुषा॑ सुभागा रा॒यस्पोषे॑ण॒ समि॒षा म॑देम ॥

### Mūla Translation

I give over to you this young bull. Move about and play with this beloved one. O cows, fortunate by birth, do not curse us. May we rejoice in increase of wealth and in food.

### Sāyaṇa

हे गावो, वो युष्मभ्यमेतं युवानं वृषभमहं परिददामि । तेन प्रियेण सह क्रीडन्तीश्चरत ।

नोऽस्मान्मा शाप्त । यतो यूयं जनुषा सुभागाः, जन्मनेव सुष्ठु भागवत्यः, ततोऽस्माकं शापो भवतीभिर्न कार्यः । किंतु तरुणं दत्तवानित्यनुग्रहः कार्यः ।

युष्माकं प्रसादाद्वयं धनपुष्ट्याऽन्नेन च संभवेम, हृष्टाः संभवेम ।

### Sāyaṇa Translation

The cows are addressed directly: “I hand over this young bull to you. Move about and play with this beloved companion.”

They are asked not to curse the sacrificer. Since they are fortunate by birth, they should respond not with a curse but with favor because a young bull has been given to them.

Through their favor, the sacrificer hopes to prosper in wealth, nourishment, and food, and to live in joy.

## नमो महिम्ने

### Saṃhitā

नमो॑ महि॒म्न उ॒त चक्षु॑षे ते॒ मरु॑तां पित॒स्तद॒हं गृ॑णामि ।
अनु॑ मन्यस्व सु॒यजा॑ यजाम॒ जुष्टं॑ दे॒वाना॑मि॒दम॑स्तु ह॒व्यम् ॥

### Mūla Translation

Homage to your greatness and to your sight, O father of the Maruts. This I proclaim to you. Approve it. May we sacrifice with a well-prepared sacrificial means. May this oblation be dear to the gods.

### Sāyaṇa

हे मरुतां पितर्देवानां जनक प्रजापते, त्वदीयाय सृष्ट्यादिरूपाय महिम्ने नमः । उतापि च चक्षुषे दृष्टिरूपाय सर्वगोचरज्ञानाय त्वदीयाय नमः ।

तन्मदीयं विवक्षितमहं गृणामि, तुभ्यं ब्रवीमि । तच्च यदुक्तं त्वमनुमन्यस्व ।

सुयजा शोभनेन यज्ञसाधनेन वृषभेण वयं यजाम । इदं वृषभरूपं हव्यं देवानां जुष्टं प्रियमस्तु ।

### Sāyaṇa Translation

Prajāpati is addressed as father of the Maruts and progenitor of the gods. Homage is offered to his greatness, especially the greatness manifested in creation, and to his all-perceiving sight.

The sacrificer asks Prajāpati to approve what is being undertaken. The bull is described as a su-yaj, a well-prepared means of sacrifice. May this bull, now functioning as the oblation, be pleasing to the gods.

## देवानामेष उपनाहः

### Saṃhitā

दे॒वाना॑मे॒ष उ॑पना॒ह आ॑सीद॒पां गर्भ॒ ओष॑धीषु॒ न्य॑क्तः ।
सोम॑स्य द्र॒प्सम॑वृणीत पू॒षा बृ॒हन्नद्रि॑रभव॒त्तदे॑षाम् ॥

### Mūla Translation

This was the bond of the gods. It became the embryo of the Waters, deposited in the plants. Pūṣan took the drop of Soma; it became their great mountain-like mass.

### Sāyaṇa

एष वृषभो देवानामुपनाहो बन्धनहेतुरासीत् । दाम्ना वलीवर्दा इवास्मिन्वृषभे देवाः प्रीत्यतिशयेन सक्ता इत्यर्थः ।

स च वृषभो हुतः सन् मेघेष्वपां संबन्धी गर्भो भूत्वा पुनरोषधीषु न्यक्तो वृष्टिरूपेणाधोमुखः पतितः ।

पूषा सोमस्य द्रप्समवृणीत, आदित्यः सलिलात्मनश्चन्द्रस्य द्रप्सं रश्मिभिर्गृहीतवान् । तञ्च द्रप्सरूपं जलं बृहन्प्रौढोऽद्रिः पर्वतसदृशो मेघोऽभवत् ।

### Sāyaṇa Translation

The bull is called the gods' upanāha, their bond, because the gods are attached to it with intense affection as oxen are tied with a rope.

Once offered into the fire, the bull becomes an embryo connected with the Waters in the clouds. It is then deposited in the plants by descending in the form of rain.

Sāyaṇa expands the imagery cosmologically. Pūṣan, understood here through Āditya, takes the drop of Soma, the water associated with the Moon, by means of his rays. That water becomes a great, mountain-like cloud.

### Notes

#### Study note: Sacrificial circulation

Bull → fire → Sun → lunar water → cloud → rain → plants.

## पिता वत्सानाम्

### Saṃhitā

पि॒ता व॒त्सानां॒ पति॑रघ्नि॒याना॒मथो॑ पि॒ता म॑ह॒तां गर्ग॑राणाम् ।
व॒त्सो ज॒रायु॑ प्रति॒धुक्पी॒यूष॑ आ॒मिक्षा॒ मस्तु॑ घृ॒त॑मस्य॒ रेतः॑ ॥

### Mūla Translation

He is father of the calves and lord of the cows that must not be harmed. He is also father of the great bellowing bulls. Calf, afterbirth, milk in the pail, colostrum, curd-mixture, whey, and ghee are his seed.

### Sāyaṇa

ये यवां वत्सास्तेषामयं वृषभः पिता, अघ्नियानां गवां पतिः । न केवलमिदानींतनानां बालानां पिता, अपि च महतां गर्गराणां पिता ।

गवामुदरे गर्भरूपो यो वत्सः, यच्च जरायुरूपं गर्भाधिकरणम्, यच्च प्रतिधुग्दोहनपात्रस्थं दुग्धम्, यच्च पीयूष ऊधस्थममृतसमानं क्षीरम्, या चामिक्षा, यच्च मस्तु, यदपि पक्वं घृतम्, तत्सर्वमस्य वृषभस्य रेतः ।

### Sāyaṇa Translation

The bull is father of the calves and lord of the aghnyā cows. He is not merely father of the present young animals, but symbolically father even of the great mature bulls.

Sāyaṇa interprets the entire reproductive and dairy sequence as a transformation of the bull's seed: the calf in the womb, placenta, milk in the pail, colostrum, curd-mixture, whey, and cooked ghee.

## त्वां गावोऽवृणत राज्याय

### Saṃhitā

त्वां गावो॑ऽवृणत रा॒ज्याय॒ त्वाꣳ ह॑वन्त म॒रुतः॑ स्व॒र्काः ।
वर्ष्म॑न् क्ष॒त्रस्य॑ क॒कुभि॑ शिश्रिया॒णस्ततो॑ न उ॒ग्रो वि भ॑जा॒ वसू॑नि ॥

### Mūla Translation

The cows chose you for kingship; the radiant Maruts call upon you. Established in the height and prominence of kṣatra, be fierce for us and distribute the riches.

### Sāyaṇa

हे वृषभ, गावः सर्वास्त्वां राज्यायावृणत, “अस्माकमयं राजा भवतु” इति वृतवत्यः ।

क्षत्त्रस्य ककुभि, ककुत्समाने प्रधानते, वर्ष्मन् शरीरं शिश्रियाणः, राजविग्रहे त्वमेव बलरूपेणावस्थितोऽसि ।

यथा वृषभस्य सर्वेषु पशुषु बलाधिक्यम्, एवं राजविग्रहस्य सर्वनियामकत्वं दृश्यते ।

### Sāyaṇa Translation

The cows choose the bull as their king.

The bull is said to dwell in the prominence of kṣatra. Sāyaṇa treats the bull's exceptional strength among animals as analogous to the king's governing power among people. Thus the bull becomes a ritual image of royal force.

## समृद्धपशु

### Saṃhitā

व्यृ॑द्धेन॒ वा ए॒ष प॒शुना॑ यजते॒ यस्यै॒तानि॒ न क्रि॒यन्ते ।
ए॒ष ह॒ वै समृ॑द्धेन यजते॒ यस्यै॒तानि॑ क्रि॒यन्ते ॥

### Mūla Translation

He whose rites are not performed in this way sacrifices with a deficient animal. He for whom they are performed sacrifices with a complete and prosperous animal.

### Sāyaṇa

यस्य यजमानस्येतान्यङ्गानि क्रियन्ते, एष यजमानः सर्वाङ्गसमृद्धेन पशुना यागं करोति ।

### Sāyaṇa Translation

The purpose of the preceding procedures is to make the animal ritually complete. When these aṅgas are performed, the yajamāna sacrifices with a paśu endowed with all its proper ritual limbs.
