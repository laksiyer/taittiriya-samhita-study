# Taittirīya Saṃhitā 3.3.7

## अनुवाकप्रवेशः

### Saṃhitā

—

### Sāyaṇa

अथ सप्तमे सोमाङ्गत्वेनाऽऽश्रावयेत्यादयो मन्त्रा विधीयन्ते —

### Sāyaṇa Translation

Now, in the seventh anuvāka, the mantras beginning with ā śrāvaya are prescribed specifically as limbs of the Soma sacrifice.

### Notes

#### Ritual: Why a fresh prescription?

Sāyaṇa will later explain that these formulas were known in the iṣṭi context, but the Soma sacrifice is not an iṣṭi-vikṛti. Therefore they are not obtained here automatically by codaka/atideśa and require an explicit prescription.

## प्रजापतिः — देवाः — असुराः — यज्ञः — छन्दांसि

### Saṃhitā

प्र॒जाप॑तिर्देवासु॒रान॑सृजत । तदनु॑ य॒ज्ञो॑ऽसृज्यत । य॒ज्ञं छन्दाꣳ॑सि । ते विष्व॑ञ्चो॒ व्य॑क्रामन् । स॒ोऽसु॑रा॒ननु॑ य॒ज्ञोऽपा᳚क्रामत् । य॒ज्ञं छन्दाꣳ॑सि ।

### Mūla Translation

Prajāpati created the gods and the Asuras. After them the sacrifice was created; after the sacrifice, the metres. The gods dispersed in different directions. The sacrifice then left them and followed the Asuras; the metres followed the sacrifice.

### Sāyaṇa

प्रजापतिरिति । कदाचित्प्रजापतिर्देवानसुरांश्च पुराऽसृजत सृष्टवान् । ताननु तेन यज्ञोऽप्यसृज्यत । यज्ञमनु छन्दांसि च ससर्ज । तदानीं ते देवा परस्परमैकमत्याभावाद्विष्वञ्चो नानागतयो भूत्वा व्यक्रामन् विविधं देशं गताः । अथ स यज्ञोऽसुराननुसृत्य देवेभ्योऽपाक्रामत् । तं च यज्ञमनुसृत्य छन्दांस्यपि देवेभ्योऽपाक्रामन् ।

### Sāyaṇa Translation

On one occasion Prajāpati first created the gods and the Asuras. After creating them, he created the sacrifice as well; following the sacrifice, he created the metres. At that time the gods, because there was no unanimity among them, became mutually divided and went off in different directions. Then the sacrifice left the gods and went after the Asuras. Following the sacrifice, the metres too left the gods.

### Notes

#### Sāyaṇa: Why did the gods lose the sacrifice?

Sāyaṇa supplies an important causal explanation not stated explicitly in the mūla: परस्परम् ऐकमत्याभावात् — because the gods lacked mutual unanimity/cohesion, they scattered.

#### Study note: Sequence

Prajāpati → gods/Asuras → sacrifice → metres. When the gods fragment, sacrifice departs; the metres follow sacrifice.

## देवानां परिभवः

### Saṃhitā

ते दे॒वा अ॑मन्यन्त — आ॒मी वा इ॒दम॑भूव॒न् यद्व॒यं स्म इति । ते प्र॒जाप॑ति॒मुपा॑धावन् ।

### Mūla Translation

The gods thought, “These Asuras have become what we once were.” They approached Prajāpati.

### Sāyaṇa

तदानीं ते देवाः परस्परमैकमत्यं प्राप्यैवं विचारितवन्तो वयं यदैश्वर्यं प्राप्य स्थिताः स्मस्तदिदं सर्वं प्राप्यासुरा एवेदानीमवस्थिता अभवन्निति विचार्य ते देवास्तथाविधं परिभवमसहमानाः प्रजापतिमुपाधावन् ।

### Sāyaṇa Translation

Then the gods regained unanimity and reflected as follows: “All the power and prosperity in which we formerly stood has now been obtained by the Asuras; they now occupy the position we once held.” Unable to endure such humiliation, the gods approached Prajāpati.

### Notes

#### Lexical: ऐश्वर्यम् / परिभवः

Sāyaṇa interprets the contrast in terms of lost ऐश्वर्य (power, lordship, prosperity) and असहमानाः परिभवम् — inability to endure their humiliation.

## छन्दसां वीर्यम्

### Saṃhitā

सोऽब्रवीत् — छन्द॑सां वी॒र्य॑मा॒दाय॒ तद्वः॒ प्र दा᳚स्यामि । स छन्द॑सां वी॒र्य॑मा॒दाय॒ तदे᳚भ्यः॒ प्राय॑च्छत् । तदनु॒ छन्दा॒ग्॒स्यपा᳚क्रामन् । छन्दाꣳ॑सि य॒ज्ञः । ततो॑ दे॒वा अभ॑वन् । परासु॑राः ।

### Mūla Translation

He said, “I shall take the potency of the metres and give it to you.” He took the potency of the metres and gave it to them. Following that potency, the metres departed from the Asuras; following the metres, the sacrifice departed. Then the gods prevailed and the Asuras were defeated.

### Sāyaṇa

अथ देवैरुपसेवितः प्रजापतिस्तान्प्रत्येवमब्रवीत् । छन्दसां वैदिकमन्त्राणां मध्ये वीर्यं सारमादाय युष्मभ्यं तत्प्रदास्यामीत्युक्त्वा तथैव कृतवान् । अथ तद्वीर्यमनु सर्वाणि छन्दांस्यसुरेभ्योऽपक्रम्य देवान्प्राप्नुवन् । तानि छन्दांस्यनुसृत्य यज्ञोऽप्यसुरेभ्योऽपक्रम्य देवान्प्राप्नोत् । ततो देवा विजयिनोऽभवन्नसुरास्तु पराभूताः ।

### Sāyaṇa Translation

Prajāpati, having been approached by the gods, said to them: “From within the chandas—the Vedic mantras—I shall take their vīrya, their essential potency (sāra), and give it to you.” Having said this, he did exactly so. Then, following that potency, all the metres withdrew from the Asuras and reached the gods. Following those metres, the sacrifice too withdrew from the Asuras and reached the gods. Thereupon the gods became victorious and the Asuras were defeated.

### Notes

#### Sāyaṇa: वीर्य = सार

छन्दसां वैदिकमन्त्राणां मध्ये वीर्यं सारम् — Sāyaṇa identifies the vīrya as the essential core/potency present within the Vedic mantras.

#### Study note: Recovery chain

वीर्य → छन्दांसि → यज्ञः → देवाः. The potency returns first; the metres follow it; sacrifice follows the metres; divine victory follows sacrifice.

## आश्रावणादिमन्त्रपञ्चकम्

### Saṃhitā

य ए॒वं छन्द॑सां वी॒र्यं॑ वेद । आ श्रा॑वय । अस्तु॒ श्रौषट् । यज । ये यजा॑महे । वषट्का॒रः । भव॑त्या॒त्मना॑ । परा᳚स्य॒ भ्रातृ॑व्यो भवति ।

### Mūla Translation

He who knows thus the potency of the metres—“Ā śrāvaya,” “Astu śrauṣaṭ,” “Yaja,” “Ye yajāmahe,” and the vaṣaṭ-call—prosperous in himself, overcomes his rival.

### Sāyaṇa

अथ मन्त्रान्विधत्ते — य एवं छन्दसामिति । यो वीर्यं वेद स स्वयमेव विजयी भवति तस्य भ्रातृव्यः पराभवति । तस्माद्वीर्यं विदित्वा प्रयुञ्जीत । आश्रावयेत्यादिमन्त्रपञ्चकं तद्वीर्यम् ।

### Sāyaṇa Translation

The text now prescribes the mantras. One who knows this vīrya becomes victorious himself, while his rival (bhrātṛvya) is defeated. Therefore these formulas should be employed with knowledge of their potency. The five mantras beginning with ā śrāvaya are themselves that vīrya.

### Notes

#### Ritual: The five formulas

आ श्रावय · अस्तु श्रौषट् · यज · ये यजामहे · वषट्. The Brāhmaṇa does not merely associate them with vīrya; it identifies this fivefold performative sequence as the vīrya of the chandas.

## इष्ट्यङ्गत्वात् सोमाङ्गत्वम्

### Saṃhitā

—

### Sāyaṇa

एतेषां मन्त्राणामर्था यो वै सप्तदशमित्यस्मिन्ननुवाके [सं. का. १ प्र. ६ अ. ११] प्रपञ्चिताः । इष्ट्यङ्गत्वेन तत्र विधिः । अत्र तु सोमाङ्गत्वेन । सोमस्येष्टिविकारत्वाभावेन चोदकात्ते मन्त्रा अप्राप्ताः ।

### Sāyaṇa Translation

The meanings of these mantras were explained in detail in the earlier anuvāka beginning yo vai saptadaśam (TS 1.6.11). There they were prescribed as components of an iṣṭi; here, however, they are prescribed as components of the Soma sacrifice. Since Soma is not a modification (vikṛti) of an iṣṭi, these mantras would not be obtained here through the codaka, the automatic transfer of ritual details from a prakṛti to its vikṛti. Hence the present explicit prescription is necessary.

### Notes

#### Mīmāṃsā: चोदक

Sāyaṇa is making a technical ritual-hermeneutic point: because Soma is not an iṣṭi-vikṛti, the iṣṭi prescription does not automatically carry over. This passage therefore has genuine vidhi-force.

#### Cross-reference: TS 1.6.11

Sāyaṇa explicitly points the reader back to the anuvāka beginning यो वै सप्तदशम् for the fuller explanation of the formulas.

## ब्रह्मवादिनः — कस्मै कम्?

### Saṃhitā

ब्रह्मवा॒दिनो॑ वदन्ति — कस्मै॒ कम॑ध्व॒र्युरा श्रा॑वय॒तीति । छन्द॑सां वी॒र्या॑येति॑ ब्रूयात् । ए॒तद्वै छन्द॑सां वी॒र्यम् — आ श्रा॑वय । अस्तु॒ श्रौषट् । यज । ये यजा॑महे । वषट्का॒रः ।

### Mūla Translation

The Brahmavādins ask, “For what purpose does the Adhvaryu perform the āśrāvaṇa?” He should answer, “For the potency of the metres.” For this indeed is the potency of the metres: ā śrāvaya, astu śrauṣaṭ, yaja, ye yajāmahe, and the vaṣaṭ-call.

### Sāyaṇa

इदानीं तानेतान्मन्त्रान्प्रशंसति — ब्रह्मवादिन इति । कस्मै कं कस्मै कामाय प्रयोजनायाध्वर्युराश्रावणं करोतीति ब्रह्मवादिभिः पृष्टो बुद्धिमानुत्तरं ब्रूयात् । प्रजापतिनोद्धृतवीर्याणां छन्दसां पुनर्वीर्यप्राप्तिलाभायाध्वर्युराश्रावयतीत्युत्तरम् । स एवोत्तरवादी वीर्यं दर्शयितुमाश्रावयेत्यादिकमनुवदति ।

### Sāyaṇa Translation

The text now praises these mantras. If the Brahmavādins ask, “For what kāma—for what purpose—does the Adhvaryu perform the āśrāvaṇa?”, an intelligent respondent should answer: “The Adhvaryu performs it so that the chandas, whose potency had been extracted by Prajāpati, may regain that potency.” The respondent then repeats ā śrāvaya and the remaining formulas in order to identify concretely what that vīrya is.

### Notes

#### Sāyaṇa: What is being restored?

Not the sacrificer's strength directly, but पुनर्वीर्यप्राप्ति of the chandas themselves—the restoration of the potency removed from them by Prajāpati.

## सवीर्यैश्छन्दोभिः

### Saṃhitā

य ए॒वं वेद॑ सवी᳚र्यैरे॒व छन्दो॑भिरर्चति॒ यत्किं चार्च॑ति ।

### Mūla Translation

He who knows thus worships whatever he worships with metres possessed of their potency.

### Sāyaṇa

इदानीं वीर्यवेदनं प्रशंसति — य एवं वेदेति । अस्मिन्यज्ञे लोकव्यवहारे वा वीर्यवेदी यत्किंचिद्देवादिकं पूजयति तत्सर्वं सवीर्यैरेव छन्दोभिः पूजितवान्भवति ।

### Sāyaṇa Translation

The text now praises knowledge of the vīrya. A person who knows this potency—whether in this sacrifice or in ordinary religious practice—whatever deity or other object he worships, is regarded as having worshipped it with chandas whose potency is fully present.

## इन्द्रस्य अमेध्यकर्म

### Saṃhitā

यदिन्द्रो॑ वृ॒त्रमह॑न् । अमे॒ध्यं तद् । यद्यती॑न॒पाव॑पत् । अमे॒ध्यं तद् । अथ॒ कस्मा॑दै॒न्द्रो य॒ज्ञ आ संस्था॑तो॒रित्या॑हुः ।

### Mūla Translation

Indra slew Vṛtra—that is impure. He cast away the Yatis—that too is impure. Why then, they ask, is the sacrifice Aindra all the way to its completion?

### Sāyaṇa

अथैतैर्मन्त्रैर्युक्तं यज्ञं प्रशंसति — यदिन्द्रो वृत्रमिति । इन्द्रो वृत्रं हतवानिति यत्तदेतद्वधरूपममेध्यमयज्ञियम् । किंचायमिन्द्रो यतीनवावपत्सालावृकेभ्यः प्रायच्छदिति यत्तदपि पापरूपत्वादयज्ञियम् । अथैवं सति कस्मात्कारणादयं यज्ञ आ संस्थातोः समाप्तिपर्यन्तमैन्द्रः संपन्न इति ब्रह्मवादिन आहुः ।

### Sāyaṇa Translation

The text now praises the sacrifice that is furnished with these mantras. Indra's slaying of Vṛtra is an act of killing and is therefore amedhya, impure, and ayajñiya, not fit for sacrifice. Likewise, Indra cast the Yatis away—Sāyaṇa explains that he handed them over to the śālāvṛkas—and this too, being sinful in character, is ayajñiya. If that is so, the Brahmavādins ask, why is this sacrifice regarded as Aindra all the way to its completion?

### Notes

#### Lexical: आ संस्थातोः

Sāyaṇa glosses this as समाप्तिपर्यन्तम् — up to the very completion of the sacrifice.

#### Study note: Problem posed

The Brāhmaṇa distinguishes mythic deeds attributed to Indra from the specific form of Indra that is ritually worshippable.

## इन्द्रस्य द्वे तनू

### Saṃhitā

इन्द्र॑स्य॒ वा ए॒षा य॒ज्ञिया॑ त॒नूर्यद्य॒ज्ञः । तामे॒व तद्य॑जन्ति ।

### Mūla Translation

The sacrifice is indeed the sacrificial form of Indra. It is that form alone which they worship.

### Sāyaṇa

तत्र बुद्धिमानेवमुत्तरं ब्रूते — इन्द्रस्य हि द्वे तन्वावयज्ञिया यज्ञिया च । राज्यं कुर्वती क्षत्त्रादीन्हिंसन्ती तनूरयज्ञिया । सा च राजसी । यज्ञ इति यदेवेन्द्रस्यैव यज्ञिया यज्ञार्हा तनूः । सा च सात्त्विकी वक्ष्यत इति । यज्ञे हविषा पूजनीयो यज्ञाङ्गदेवतारूपः सात्त्विकविग्रह इत्यर्थः । तत्तस्मिन्यज्ञे तामेव सात्त्विकीं यज्ञार्हां तनूं यजमानाः प्रयजन्ति ।

### Sāyaṇa Translation

The knowledgeable respondent gives the following answer. Indra has two forms (tanū): one is ayajñiya, not sacrificially fit, and the other is yajñiya, fit for sacrifice. The form that exercises kingship and injures Kṣatriyas and others is the non-sacrificial form; that form is rājasic. But what is called “the sacrifice” is precisely Indra's yajñiya tanū—his form worthy of sacrifice; that form is sāttvic. In other words, in the sacrifice Indra is worshipped with oblations as a deity belonging to the sacrificial complex, in a sāttvic form. Therefore the sacrificers worship only that sāttvic form of Indra which is fit for sacrifice.

### Notes

#### Theology: Two forms of Indra

अयज्ञिया तनूः = राजसी; यज्ञिया तनूः = सात्त्विकी. This guṇa-language is Sāyaṇa's explicit explanatory framework.

#### Study note: यज्ञः = इन्द्रस्य यज्ञिया तनूः

The identification is not 'everything Indra does is sacrificial'; it is the opposite. The ritual selects the deity's yajña-fit form.

## फलश्रुतिः

### Saṃhitā

य ए॒वं वेद॑ । उपै॑नं य॒ज्ञो न॑मति ॥

### Mūla Translation

He who knows thus—the sacrifice inclines toward him.

### Sāyaṇa

यज्ञरूपतनुवदेनं प्रशंसति — य एवमिति ।

### Sāyaṇa Translation

By teaching that the sacrifice is itself Indra's sacrificial form, the text praises the person who knows this teaching.

## विनियोगसंग्रहः

### Saṃhitā

—

### Sāyaṇa

आश्रावयेत्यादयोऽत्र पञ्च मन्त्रा उदीरिताः । आश्रावणादि सोमार्थमत्र प्राक्तनमिष्टिगम् ॥

### Sāyaṇa Translation

The five mantras beginning with ā śrāvaya are taught here. The āśrāvaṇa and the following formulas, which previously belonged to the iṣṭi context, are here prescribed for the Soma sacrifice.

### Notes

#### Editorial: Use of this page

This vinियोगसंग्रह is retained rather than silently omitted because it tells us exactly how Sāyaṇa understands the ritual function of the anuvāka.
