# Taittirīya Saṃhitā 3.3.5

## अनुवाकप्रवेशः

### Saṃhitā

—

### Sāyaṇa

चतुर्थेऽंशोरदाभ्यस्य मन्त्रव्याख्यानमीरितम् ।
अथ पञ्चमोऽग्निष्टोमशेषं परित्यज्य विकृतिरूपस्य द्वादशाहस्य शेषाः पृश्नीग्रहा उच्यन्ते ।

### Sāyaṇa Translation

In the fourth anuvāka the explanation of the mantras belonging to Aṃśu and Adābhya was given. Now, in the fifth, leaving aside the remaining Agniṣṭoma material, the Pṛśni grahas belonging to the Dvādaśāha, which is a vikṛti, are taught.

## पृश्नीग्रहाणामुन्मानम्

### Saṃhitā

वा॒युर॑सि प्रा॒णो नाम॑ सवि॒तुराधि॑पत्येऽपा॒नं मे॑ दाः ।
चक्षु॑रसि॒ श्रोत्रं॒ नाम॑ धा॒तुराधि॑पत्य॒ आयु॑र्मे दाः ।
रू॒पम॑सि॒ वर्णो॒ नाम॒ बृह॒स्पते॒राधि॑पत्ये प्र॒जां मे॑ दाः ।
ऋ॒तम॑सि स॒त्यं नामेन्द्र॒स्याधि॑पत्ये क्ष॒त्रं मे॑ दाः ।
भू॒तम॑सि॒ भव्यं॒ नाम॑ पितृ॒णामाधि॑पत्ये॒ऽपामोष॑धीनां॒ गर्भं॑ धाः ।

### Mūla Translation

You are Vāyu, named Prāṇa. Under Savitṛ's overlordship give me apāna. You are sight, named hearing. Under Dhātṛ give me life. You are form, named complexion. Under Bṛhaspati give me progeny. You are ṛta, named satya. Under Indra give me kṣatra. You are what has been, named what is to be. Under the Fathers establish the embryo of Waters and plants.

### Sāyaṇa

कल्पः — “प्राकृतं यजुर्मानमनुद्रुत्य वायुरसि प्राणो नामेति दशभिः पृश्नीग्रहाणां मन्त्रैर्दश मानानि मिमीते” इति ।

इन्द्राय त्वा वृत्रघ्न इत्यादीनि यजूंषि सोमोन्मानकरणानि । तदेतद्यजुर्जातमुच्चार्य वायुरसीत्यादिभिर्मन्त्रैः सोमस्योन्मानं कुर्यात् ।

वायुरसि प्राण इति । हे सोम, त्वं यजमानेन पीतः सञ्शरीरमध्ये धारणादिना वायोराप्यायनकारित्वाद्वायुरसि । सामान्याकारेण वायुर्भूत्वा विशेषाकारेण प्राणो नामासि । बहिर्निर्गमनशीलस्त्वमुच्छ्वासरूपोऽसि । तादृशस्त्वं सवितुः प्रेरकस्य परमेश्वरस्याऽऽधिपत्ये स्थित्वा मे मह्यमपानमन्तःप्रवेशवन्तं वायुविशेषं दा देहि ।

तथा चक्षुःश्रोत्रयोरप्यायनकारित्वात्तदुभयरूपोऽसि । अतो धातुर्देहेन्द्रियादिस्रष्टुराधिपत्ये स्थित्वा मह्यमायुर्देहि ।

तथा शरीरावयवसौष्ठवलक्षणस्य रूपस्य कान्तिलक्षणस्य वर्णस्य च हेतुत्वात्तदुभयरूपोऽसि । अतस्तदुभयकारिणो बृहस्पतेराधिपत्ये स्थित्वा मह्यं पुत्रपौत्रादिरूपां प्रजां देहि ।

तथा मनसा चिन्त्यमानस्य ऋतस्य वाचोच्चार्यमाणस्य सत्यस्य च हेतुत्वात्तदुभयरूपोऽसि । अतस्तदुभयपालकस्येन्द्रस्याऽऽधिपत्ये स्थित्वा मह्यं बलं देहि ।

तथा शरीरमध्ये यद्भूतं पूर्वं सिद्धं धातुवैषम्यं यच्च भव्यमितः परं भविष्यत्तदुभयसमाधानहेतुत्वात्तदुभयरूपोऽसि । अतस्तदुभयसमाधातॄणां पितॄणामाधिपत्ये स्थित्वाऽऽपामोषधीनां च संबन्धी यो गर्भः पशुरूपस्तं धाः संपादय ।

### Sāyaṇa Translation

The Kalpa says that after reciting the ordinary Yajus used for measuring Soma, the priest measures ten portions with the ten Pṛśni-graha mantras beginning vāyur asi prāṇo nāma.

On the first mantra, Sāyaṇa says: O Soma, when drunk by the yajamāna you nourish the vital air within the body, and therefore you are Vāyu. In the general form you are Vāyu, while in a specific form your name is Prāṇa. You are the outward-moving breath. Standing under the overlordship of Savitṛ, the divine impeller, give me apāna, the particular vital air that moves inward.

Because Soma nourishes sight and hearing, it is of the nature of both. Under Dhātṛ, creator of body and senses, it is asked to give life.

Because Soma is the cause of bodily proportion and form, and also of luster or complexion, it is identified with rūpa and varṇa. Under Bṛhaspati it is asked to give progeny.

Because Soma is the cause both of ṛta, truth conceived in the mind, and satya, truth uttered in speech, it is of the nature of both. Under Indra it is asked to give strength, here corresponding to kṣatra.

Finally, Soma is said to remedy both bodily imbalance that has already arisen and imbalance that may arise in the future. Under the Fathers it is asked to establish the embryo connected with Waters and plants, which Sāyaṇa identifies as cattle.

### Notes

#### Study note: ऋत and सत्य

ऋत = truth mentally conceived.

सत्य = truth verbally expressed.

## ऋतस्य पञ्च रूपाणि

### Saṃhitā

ऋ॒तस्य॑ त्वा॒ व्यो॑मन ।
ऋ॒तस्य॑ त्वा॒ विभू॑मन ।
ऋ॒तस्य॑ त्वा॒ विध॑र्मण ।
ऋ॒तस्य॑ त्वा स॒त्याय ।
ऋ॒तस्य॑ त्वा॒ ज्योति॑षे ।

### Mūla Translation

I measure you for the expanse of ṛta, for the wide manifestation of ṛta, for the sustaining order of ṛta, for the truth of ṛta, and for the light of ṛta.

### Sāyaṇa

हे सोम, ऋतस्य व्योमने सत्यस्य विशेषेण रक्षणाय त्वां मिमे । ऋतस्य विभूमने सत्यस्य विशेषेण बाहुल्याय त्वां मिमे । ऋतस्य विधर्मणे सत्यस्य विशेषेण धारणाय त्वां मिमे । ऋतस्य सत्याय सत्यस्य सत्याय प्रमादरूपानृतराहित्याय त्वां मिमे । ऋतस्य ज्योतिषे सत्यस्य प्रकाशाय त्वां मिमे ।

### Sāyaṇa Translation

For the vyoman of ṛta means for the special protection of truth. For the vibhūman of ṛta means for the abundant expansion of truth. For the vidharman of ṛta means for the sustaining of truth. For the satya of ṛta means for truth free from falsehood arising through error. For the jyotis of ṛta means for the illumination of truth.

## विराजः उत्पत्तिः

### Saṃhitā

प्र॒जाप॑तिर्वि॒राज॑मपश्य॒त्तया॑ भू॒तं च॒ भव्यं॑ चासृजत॒ तामृषि॑भ्यस्ति॒रो॑ऽदधा॒त्तां ज॒मद॑ग्नि॒स्तप॑सापश्य॒त्तया॒ वै स पृश्नी॒न्कामा॑नसृजत॒ तत्पृश्नी॑नां पृश्नि॒त्वम् ।

### Mūla Translation

Prajāpati perceived the Virāj. With it he created what has been and what is to be. He concealed it from the ṛṣis. Jamadagni perceived it through tapas. With it he created the Pṛśni desires. That is why they are called Pṛśni.

### Sāyaṇa

प्रजापतिः पूर्वं विचार्य सृष्टेः साधनभूतां विराजमपश्यत् । वायुरसीत्यादिमन्त्रसमष्टिर्दशसंख्योपेतत्वाद्दशाक्षरयुक्तच्छन्दःसाम्येन विराडित्युच्यते ।

तया च विराजा भूतं भविष्यच्च जगदसृजत । ततस्तां विराजमृषिभ्यो न प्रकाशितवान् । तदा जमदग्निस्तपः कृत्वा प्रजापत्यनुग्रहेण तां विराजमपश्यत् ।

तया विराजा स जमदग्निः पृश्नीन्धेनुस्वरूपान्कामान्भोगानसृजत ।

पृश्नयः कामधेनवः सृष्टा यैर्मन्त्रैस्ते मन्त्राः पृश्नय इति निर्वक्तुं शक्यत्वात् ।

### Sāyaṇa Translation

Prajāpati perceived Virāj as the means of creation. The aggregate of the ten mantras beginning vāyur asi is called Virāj because it consists of ten members and corresponds to the ten-syllabled Virāj metre.

With this Virāj he created the world, both what exists and what is to come. He concealed it from the ṛṣis. Jamadagni perceived it through tapas and the favor of Prajāpati.

With this Virāj Jamadagni created desired enjoyments having the form of Pṛśni cows.

Sāyaṇa gives the nirvacana: those mantras by which the Pṛśnis, the kāmadhenus, were created are themselves called Pṛśnis.

### Notes

#### Nirvacana: पृश्नी

Sāyaṇa connects Pṛśni with कामधेनु and desired enjoyments.

## ग्रहणम् = उन्मानम्

### Saṃhitā

यत्पृश्न॑यो गृ॒ह्यन्ते॒ पृश्नी॑ने॒व तैः कामा॒न् यज॑मा॒नोऽव॑ रुंधे ।

### Mūla Translation

When the Pṛśnis are taken, by them the yajamāna secures the Pṛśni desires.

### Sāyaṇa

ग्रहणं नाम न पात्रेषु सोमरसस्य धारणं किंतु नद्धस्य सोमस्योन्मानम् ।

तैरुन्मानलक्षणैर्ग्रहैर्यजमानः कामधेनुसदृशान्भोगान्प्राप्नोति ।

### Sāyaṇa Translation

Here grahaṇa does not mean placing Soma juice into vessels. It means measuring portions of the bound Soma.

Through these grahas, whose defining act is measurement, the yajamāna obtains enjoyments like those produced by a kāmadhenu.

### Notes

#### Ritual: ग्रहण

Here ग्रहण means उन्मान, measuring Soma.

## प्रथमपञ्चकस्य फलानि

### Saṃhitā

वा॒युर॑सि प्रा॒णो नामेत्या॑ह प्राणापा॒नावे॒वाव॑ रुंधे ।
चक्षु॑रसि॒ श्रोत्रं॒ नामेत्या॒हायु॑रे॒वाव॑ रुंधे ।
रू॒पम॑सि॒ वर्णो॒ नामेत्या॑ह प्र॒जामे॒वाव॑ रुंधे ।
ऋ॒तम॑सि स॒त्यं नामेत्या॑ह क्ष॒त्रमे॒वाव॑ रुंधे ।
भू॒तम॑सि॒ भव्यं॒ नामेत्या॑ह प॒शूने॒वाव॑ रुंधे ।

### Mūla Translation

By the first he secures prāṇa and apāna. By the second, life. By the third, progeny. By the fourth, kṣatra. By the fifth, cattle.

### Sāyaṇa

प्रथममन्त्रेण प्राणापानपोषणलक्षणं कामं दर्शयति । द्वितीयमन्त्रेण चक्षुःश्रोत्रस्थैर्यहेतोरायुषः प्राप्तिं दर्शयति । तृतीयमन्त्रेणावयवसौष्ठवकान्तिभ्यामुपेतायाः प्रजायाः संपत्तिं दर्शयति । चतुर्थमन्त्रेण मानसवाचिकसत्यसाधनस्य संपत्तिं दर्शयति । पञ्चममन्त्रेण भूतभविष्यदस्वास्थ्यपरिहारहेतूनां पशूनां प्राप्तिं दर्शयति ।

अपामोषधीनां गर्भं धा इति तस्मिन्मन्त्रे समाम्नातम् । तत्र तृणोदकपरिणामरूपत्वात्पशव एव तादृशो गर्भ इह विवक्षितः ।

एतावद्वा इति । प्राणापानस्वास्थ्यप्रभृति पशुप्राप्तिपर्यन्तं यावत्फलमुक्तमेतावदेव पुरुषं परितो वर्तते ।

### Sāyaṇa Translation

The first mantra gives nourishment of prāṇa and apāna. The second gives life, supporting sight and hearing. The third gives progeny endowed with bodily form and beauty. The fourth gives the means of mental and verbal truth. The fifth gives cattle.

The “embryo of Waters and plants” is cattle because cattle arise through the transformation of grass and water.

The Brāhmaṇa then summarizes that these results, from healthy breath through cattle, encompass what a person requires on every side.

## उत्तरपञ्चकम्: लोकजयः

### Saṃhitā

ऋ॒तस्य॑ त्वा॒ व्यो॑मन॒ इत्या॑हे॒यं वा ऋ॒तस्य॒ व्यो॑मे॒मामे॒वाभि ज॑यति ।
ऋ॒तस्य॑ त्वा॒ विभू॑मन॒ इत्या॑हा॒न्तरि॑क्षं॒ वा ऋ॒तस्य॒ विभू॑मा॒न्तरि॑क्षमे॒वाभि ज॑यति ।
ऋ॒तस्य॑ त्वा॒ विध॑र्मण॒ इत्या॑ह॒ द्यौर्वा ऋ॒तस्य॒ विध॑र्म॒ दिव॑मे॒वाभि ज॑यति ।
ऋ॒तस्य॑ त्वा स॒त्यायेत्या॑ह॒ दिशो॒ वा ऋ॒तस्य॑ स॒त्यं दिश॑ ए॒वाभि ज॑यति ।
ऋ॒तस्य॑ त्वा॒ ज्योति॑ष॒ इत्या॑ह सुव॒र्गो वै लो॒क ऋ॒तस्य॒ ज्योतिः॑ सुव॒र्गमे॒व लो॒कम॒भि ज॑यति ।

### Mūla Translation

The latter five formulas are mapped onto earth, atmosphere, heaven, the directions, and the heavenly world. By them the sacrificer conquers those worlds.

### Sāyaṇa

उत्तरस्मिन्मन्त्रपञ्चके व्योमन इत्यादिभिः पञ्चभिश्चतुर्थ्यन्तपदैर्लोकत्रयस्य दिशां स्वर्गस्य च विवक्षितत्वात्तद्विजयः फलम् ।

ऋतस्य त्वेति । ऋतशब्दः सत्यवाची ।

### Sāyaṇa Translation

The five dative expressions beginning with vyoman indicate the three worlds, the directions, and the heavenly world. Their result is conquest of those realms. Here ṛta denotes satya.

## दश संख्या, विराज्, अन्नम्

### Saṃhitā

दश॒ संप॑द्यन्ते । दशा᳚क्षरा वि॒राट् । अन्नं॑ वि॒राट् । वि॒राज्ये॒वान्नाद्ये॒ प्रति॑ तिष्ठति ॥

### Mūla Translation

They amount to ten. Virāj has ten syllables. Virāj is food. He becomes established in Virāj and in food.

### Sāyaṇa

कामप्रापके पूर्वमन्त्रपञ्चके लोकजयहेतावुत्तरमन्त्रपञ्चके चावस्थितां संख्यां प्रशंसति — दश सम् इति ।

### Sāyaṇa Translation

The first five produce desired human attainments. The latter five produce conquest of the worlds. Together they make ten, corresponding to Virāj, which is identified with food.

### Notes

#### Structure: 5 + 5

First five: human completeness.

Second five: cosmic conquest.

Ten: Virāj.

Virāj: food.

## Integrated Detailed Study Notes

### NOTE

This anuvāka is organized around **two sets of five**.

The **first five** concern the completeness of embodied human life:

- prāṇa and apāna
- life through sight and hearing
- progeny through bodily form and complexion
- kṣatra through ṛta and satya
- cattle as the “embryo of Waters and plants”

The Saṃhitā then sums this up:

**एतावद्वै पुरुषं परितः**

“This indeed is all that surrounds a person.”

Sāyaṇa understands this literally as the total range of results required by a human being.

The **second five** turn outward from the person to the cosmos:

- earth
- atmosphere
- heaven
- directions
- heavenly world

Thus the ten Pṛśni-grahas move from **human completeness to cosmic conquest**.

There is also an elegant numerical structure:

**5 human attainments + 5 cosmic attainments = 10**

and:

**10 mantras = the 10-syllabled Virāj**

This is why Prajāpati's discovery of the Virāj becomes central to the Brāhmaṇa explanation.

Sāyaṇa's account of the name **Pṛśni** is particularly important. He does not simply explain it as “variegated.” Instead he connects the ten mantras with the **Pṛśni cows**, understood as wish-fulfilling cows:

**पृश्नयः कामधेनवः**

The mantras are called Pṛśni because Jamadagni, through the Virāj constituted by these ten mantras, created desired enjoyments having the nature of such cows.

So in Sāyaṇa's interpretation:

**Pṛśni-grahas → Pṛśni/Kāmadhenu → kāmāḥ → desired enjoyments**

The name is therefore tied directly to the **fruit-producing power** of these ten Soma measurements.

**इति श्रीमत्सायणाचार्यविरचिते माधवीये वेदार्थप्रकाशे कृष्णयजुर्वेदीयतैत्तिरीयसंहिताभाष्ये तृतीयकाण्डे तृतीयप्रपाठके पञ्चमोऽनुवाकः ॥५॥**
