# अथर्ववेदीयसन्ध्योपासनम्

## Primary text now available

[[../sources/atharvaveda/Atharvaveda-Parisista-41-Sandhyopasana|Atharvavedapariśiṣṭa 41, Sandhyopāsanavidhi]]

This text describes preparation, orientation, water offerings, Savitṛ and solar mantras, Gāyatrījapa, and timing. It must be studied as an Atharvavedic **Pariśiṣṭa**, rather than attributed to the Kauśikasūtra or the Atharvaveda Saṃhitā.
