# अथर्ववेदीयसन्ध्यावन्दनप्रयोगः

**शौनकशाखा | Working scholarly prayoga, Devanāgarī edition, 24 November 2026**

> [!important] Status
> This is a *substantial first working edition*, **not yet a certified complete or recitation-ready manual**. It follows Atharvavedapariśiṣṭa 41 as the procedural source. **Accented Saṃhitā:** [GRETIL/TITUS Śaunaka recension](https://druvx13.github.io/GRETIL-mirror/gretil/1_sanskr/1_veda/1_sam/avs_acu.htm)

## Editorial priorities

1. The passage calls for morning, midday and evening mantras in 41.1, while 41.3.6 and 41.3.8 emphasize morning and evening practice. This is an internal textual difference, not a license to erase the midday references.
2. `iti` marks mantra incipits rather than necessarily complete texts; a *pratīka* does not alone determine whether the full hymn, verse or fragment is performed.
3. The Pariśiṣṭa describes `vīrāsana` as an upright-knee sitting position (41.2.1). Avoid importing the meaning of the modern yoga pose.
4. Where the precise action or recitational extent is unresolved, the procedure marks it *to verify*, rather than specifying an invented gesture or count.
5. **Devanāgarī is the principal display script.** The source’s accented IAST reading is retained under each mantra. Devanāgarī svaras are not asserted yet: they require separate edition-specific collation, because Roman acute accents cannot be mapped mechanically to Vedic Devanāgarī notation.

## प्रयोगक्रमः (sequential outline)

| Order | Action | Pariśiṣṭa | Instruction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | शौचम् / देशनिर्णयः | 41.1.2–3 | Proceed eastward or northward out of the village to a clean, sheltered or sacred place; wash hands, feet, face |
| 2 | आसनम् | 41.1.3, 41.2.1 | Sit facing east in the text's `vīrāsana` posture; its raised-knee definition is given later |
| 3 | आचमनम् | 41.1.3 | Sip water with `jīvā stha` |
| 4 | अभ्युक्षणम् | 41.1.3 | Sprinkle with `āpo hi ṣṭhā` |
| 5 | प्राणायामः | 41.1.3 | Perform prāṇāyāma; the text does not here specify a count, ratio, or full mantra |
| 6 | पुनराचमनम् | 41.1.3 | Sip again |
| 7 | जलोत्सर्गः | 41.1.3 | Stand; release water held in the right hand with `ayojālā`, `bahudhā` (in many streams/ways; method to verify) |
| 8 | सौरमन्त्राः | 41.1.4–8 | Recite the time-specific mantras below, with optional solar mantras (`yathākāmam`) |
| 9 | सावित्रीजपः | 41.1.9; 41.2.4–7 | Invoke Gāyatrī; meditate on deity, metre, seer and syllables; utter mahāvyāhṛtis and perform Sāvitrījapa |
| 10 | जपसङ्ख्या | 41.2.7–13 | Counts specified: 8, 11, 12, 15, 100, 1000, with corresponding stated fruits |
| 11 | आशीर्वचनानि | 41.3.1 | Recite benedictory `paśyema śaradaḥ śatam`, `indra jīva` etc. |
| 12 | विसर्जनम् / उपस्थानम् | 41.3.2 | Dismiss with `stutā mayā varadā` and perform indicated upasthāna mantras |

## Time-specific sequence

### प्रातःसन्ध्या

Follow common steps 1–7. AVParis 41.1.4 names **`hariḥ suparṇa` (AVŚ 19.65.1)** for morning. The text also allows other solar mantras in 41.1.6 and mentions `ud vayaṃ tamasas pari` at 41.1.7; its exact placement needs collation. Invoke Gāyatrī and perform Sāvitrījapa as instructed in 41.2, followed by the benedictions and dismissal.

### माध्याह्निकम्

Follow the applicable common procedures. AVParis 41.1.5 names **`ud u tyaṃ` and `citraṃ devānām`** at midday. The passage's distinction between the two incipits should be preserved. AVParis 41.3.6–8 places emphasis on the morning/evening Sandhyās, so the historical status of a fully independent midday rite should remain a comparative research question.

### सायंसन्ध्या

The text specifies **`ud ghed abhi śrutāmagham` (AVŚ 20.7.1)** at sunset while sitting (41.1.8). The earlier actions, Sāvitrījapa and conclusions are to be connected in the sequence specified by the Pariśiṣṭa, without importing an unrelated later sandhyā format.

## सावित्री, व्याहृतयः, तथा जपसङ्ख्या

AVParis 41.2.4: `āyātu varadā` for invocation; 41.2.5 gives Gāyatrī as chandas, Savitṛ as devatā, Viśvāmitra as ṛṣi; 41.2.6 prescribes mental contemplation and mahāvyāhṛtis before Gāyatrījapa. The exact full Śaunaka text of the invocation and the desired Sāvitrī reading require edition-specific collation. We must not automatically import the Taittirīya svara pattern.

| Count | Explanatory correspondence in 41.2.8–13 |
|---:|---|
| 8 | Gāyatrī metre, attainment of earth world |
| 11 | Triṣṭubh, intermediate world |
| 12 | Jagatī, heaven |
| 15 | Fifteenfold vajra, brahmaloka |
| 100 | Hundred-jointed vajra, all worlds |
| 1000 | Fruits associated with rites including Agniṣṭoma and Aptoryāma |

*These correspondences are the text's explanations, not a modern instruction to perform all counts successively.*

## Other cited mantras requiring exact identification

- **41.2.4 `āyātu varadā`:** full invocation text and recension-specific variants; later ritual books preserve differing readings.
- **41.3.2 `udīrāṇā`, `uta sūryasya`, `āvṛtam asapatnam purastāt`:** verify exact mantra boundaries and Śaunaka Saṃhitā references.
- **41.1.5 `ud u tyaṃ / citraṃ devānām`:** compare AVŚ 13.2.35, AVŚ 20.107.14 and the possibly associated solar verses, while confirming the precise wording of the Pariśiṣṭa.
- **41.1.9 `sāvitryante vāñjalayo japaś ca`:** grammar, textual variant and ritual placement require further verification.
- **41.4–6:** timing, eulogies of Sandhyā, and Sāvitrī's symbolic body belong in the separate *explanatory study*, not as extra physical actions in the daily procedure.
