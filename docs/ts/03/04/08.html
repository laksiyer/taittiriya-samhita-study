Taittirīya Saṃhitā 3.4.8

Dense study edition | kāmya applications of Rāṣṭrabhṛts

अनुवाकप्रवेशः

Saṃhitā
Sāyaṇa

अनुवाके सप्तमेऽस्मिन्नुक्ता राष्ट्रभृतोऽखिलाः ।
अथाष्टमे तेषां काम्याः प्रयोगा उच्यन्ते ।

Sāyaṇa translation / explanation

The seventh anuvāka gave the Rāṣṭrabhṛt mantras themselves. The eighth teaches their kāmya applications.

राष्ट्रकामः

Saṃhitā

रा॒ष्ट्रका॑माय होत॒व्या॑ ।
रा॒ष्ट्रं वै रा᳚ष्ट्र॒भृतः ।
रा॒ष्ट्रेणै॒वास्मै॑ रा॒ष्ट्रमव॑ रुंधे ।
रा॒ष्ट्रमे॒व भ॑वति ॥

Mūla translation

They should be offered for one who desires a realm. The Rāṣṭrabhṛts are the realm. By means of the realm he secures a realm for him. He comes to possess a realm.

Sāyaṇa

राष्ट्रभृत्संज्ञका मन्त्रास्तेषां राष्ट्रप्राप्तिहेतुत्वाद्राष्ट्रमित्यपचर्यते । तन्मन्त्रहोमलक्षणेन राष्ट्रेणैव यजमानार्थं भूमिविशेषरूपं राष्ट्रं संपादयति ।

Sāyaṇa translation / explanation

Because the Rāṣṭrabhṛt mantras produce acquisition of a realm, they are themselves figuratively called rāṣṭra. The ritual realm is used to secure the concrete territorial realm.

आत्मोत्कर्षः

Saṃhitā

आ॒त्मने॑ होत॒व्या॑ ।
रा॒ष्ट्रं प्र॒जा रा॒ष्ट्रं प॒शवो॑ रा॒ष्ट्रं यच्छ्रेष्ठो॒ भव॑ति ।
वसि॑ष्ठः समा॒नानां᳚ भवति ॥

Mūla translation

They should be offered for oneself. Progeny are a realm, cattle are a realm, and superiority is a realm. He becomes the most eminent among his equals.

Sāyaṇa

भूमिविशेषरूपे राष्ट्रे प्रजापशुश्रैष्ठ्यानामभावे सति भोगासंभवात्, भूमिवद्भोगहेतुत्वेन प्रजादीनामपि राष्ट्रत्वम् ।

Sāyaṇa translation / explanation

Territory alone does not constitute usable sovereignty. Progeny, cattle, and social superiority are also called rāṣṭra because they make the enjoyment of rule possible.

ग्रामकामः

Saṃhitā

ग्राम॑कामाय होत॒व्या॑ ।
अधि॒देव॑ने जुहोति ।
त ए॑न॒मव॑रुद्धा॒ उप॑ तिष्ठन्ते ॥

Mūla translation

They should be offered for one desiring a village. He offers at the gaming place. Those whom he has brought under control attend upon him.

Sāyaṇa
Sāyaṇa translation / explanation

The village is understood through one's co-resident kin. The offering is made at the gaming place, a place of social gathering, so that one's sajātas become subject and attentive.

ओजस्कामः

Saṃhitā

रथमु॒ख ओज॑स्कामस्य होत॒व्या॑ ।
ओजो॒ वै रा᳚ष्ट्र॒भृत॒ ओजो॒ रथः ॥

Mūla translation

For one desiring strength, they should be offered at the front of the chariot. The Rāṣṭrabhṛts are strength; the chariot is strength.

Sāyaṇa
Sāyaṇa translation / explanation

For one desiring strength, the offering is made at the front of the chariot. The Rāṣṭrabhṛts and the chariot are both treated as embodiments of ojas.

राष्ट्रभ्रष्टस्य पुनराप्तिः

Saṃhitā

यो रा॒ष्ट्रादप॑भूतः॒ स्यात्तस्मै॑ होत॒व्या॑ ।
याव॑न्तोऽस्य॒ रथाः॒ स्युस्तान्ब्रू॑याद्यु॒ङ्घ्वमिति ।
रा॒ष्ट्रमे॒वास्मै॑ युनक्ति ॥

Mūla translation

For one who has been deprived of his realm, these should be offered. He should command all his chariots, “Yoke them.” Thus he yokes the realm to him.

Sāyaṇa
Sāyaṇa translation / explanation

For a ruler deprived of his realm, the surviving chariots are ordered to be yoked. The physical yoking becomes a ritual image of re-yoking sovereignty to the ruler.

संग्रामे

Saṃhitā

संग्रा॒मे संय॑त्ते होत॒व्या॑ ।
यस्य॒ पूर्व॑स्य॒ जुह्व॑ति॒ स ए॒व भ॑वति॒ जय॑ति॒ तꣳ संग्रा॒मम् ॥

Mūla translation

When battle is joined, these should be offered. The one for whom they are offered first gains the advantage and wins that battle.

Sāyaṇa
Sāyaṇa translation / explanation

When battle is joined, the one for whom the Rāṣṭrabhṛts are offered first gains the advantage and wins the contest.

उन्मादपरिहारः

Saṃhitā

य उ॒न्माद्ये॒त्तस्मै॑ होत॒व्या॑ ।
गन्धर्वाप्स॒रसो॒ वा ए॒तमुन्मा॑दयन्ति ।
तस्मै॒ स्वाहा॒ ताभ्यः॒ स्वाहेति॑ जुहोति ॥

Mūla translation

For one who becomes deranged, these should be offered. Gandharvas and Apsarases cause him to become deranged. He offers, “Svāhā to him; svāhā to them.”

Sāyaṇa

राष्ट्रभृन्मन्त्राश्च गन्धर्वाप्सरसस्तद्देवत्यत्वात् । तेन होमेनोभयविधानुन्मादयितॄन् शान्तान्करोति ।

Sāyaṇa translation / explanation

Because the Rāṣṭrabhṛt mantras are addressed to Gandharvas and Apsarases, the same theology is used here in a therapeutic context to pacify those beings when they are believed to cause derangement.

प्रतिलोमहोमः

Saṃhitā

अभि॒चर॑ता प्रतिलो॒मꣳ हो॑त॒व्याः᳚ ॥

Mūla translation

One performing an abhicāra rite should offer them in reverse order.

Sāyaṇa

प्रतिलोममन्त्यादिक्रमेण अस्याभिचरितस्य प्राणान्प्रतीचः प्राणापानादीन्विपरीतस्थानैः प्रतियौति ।

Sāyaṇa translation / explanation

Sāyaṇa explains the hostile rite through reversal: the mantras are offered in reverse order, symbolically reversing the victim's vital functions.

Study note

This is preserved as historical ritual description and interpretation, not as procedural guidance.

ज्येष्ठबन्धुः

Saṃhitā
Sāyaṇa

वर्ष्म वै राष्ट्रभृतः, वर्ष्म स्थलं । वर्ष्मणैवैनं वर्ष्म समानानां गमयति ।

Sāyaṇa translation / explanation

Raised ground symbolizes elevated social standing. Seating the displaced senior there ritually restores him to prominence among his equals.
