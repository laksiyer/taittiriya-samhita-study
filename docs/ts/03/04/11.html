𑌸𑍁॒𑌗𑌾॒𑌰𑍍॒𑌹॒𑌪॒𑌤𑍍𑌯𑌾𑌃 𑌸𑌮𑌿𑌷𑍋᳴ 𑌦𑌿𑌦𑍀𑌹𑍍𑌯𑌸𑍍𑌮॒𑌦𑍍𑌰𑌿𑌯॒𑌕𑍍𑌸𑌂𑌮𑌿᳴𑌮𑍀𑌹𑌿॒ 𑌶𑍍𑌰𑌵𑌾𑍞᳴𑌸𑌿 ॥

Mūla translation

O Agni, god, you grant great nourishment or long life to the giver. You are wise, young, and lord of the house. Agni, carrier of oblations, ageless, our father, pervasive and radiant, of beautiful appearance, shine for us with foods that establish good household lordship, and bring worthy fame toward us.

𑌦𑌾𑌶𑍁𑌷𑍇 𑌹𑌵𑌿𑌰𑍍𑌦𑌤𑍍𑌤𑌵𑌤𑍇 𑌯𑌜𑌮𑌾𑌨𑌾𑌯 𑌬𑍃𑌹𑌦𑍍𑌵𑌯𑍋 𑌦𑌧𑌾𑌸𑌿, 𑌮𑌹𑌦𑌨𑍍𑌨𑌂 𑌦𑍀𑌰𑍍𑌘𑌾𑌮𑌾𑌯𑍁𑌰𑍍𑌵𑌾 𑌪𑍍𑌰𑌯𑌚𑍍𑌛𑌸𑌿 । 𑌕𑌵𑌿𑌰𑍍𑌵𑌿𑌦𑍍𑌵𑌾𑌨𑍍 । 𑌗𑍃𑌹𑌪𑌤𑌿𑌰𑍍𑌗𑍃𑌹𑌸𑍍𑌯 𑌪𑌾𑌲𑌯𑌿𑌤𑌾 । 𑌯𑍁𑌵𑌾 𑌨𑌿𑌤𑍍𑌯𑌤𑌰𑍁𑌣𑌃 ।

దాశుషే హవిర్దత్తవతే యజమానాయ బృహద్వయో దధాసి, మహదన్నం దీర్ఘామాయుర్వా ప్రయచ్ఛసి . కవిర్విద్వాన్ . గృహపతిర్గృహస్య పాలయితా . యువా నిత్యతరుణః .

ದಾಶುಷೇ ಹವಿರ್ದತ್ತವತೇ ಯಜಮಾನಾಯ ಬೃಹದ್ವಯೋ ದಧಾಸಿ, ಮಹದನ್ನಂ ದೀರ್ಘಾಮಾಯುರ್ವಾ ಪ್ರಯಚ್ಛಸಿ . ಕವಿರ್ವಿದ್ವಾನ್ . ಗೃಹಪತಿರ್ಗೃಹಸ್ಯ ಪಾಲಯಿತಾ . ಯುವಾ ನಿತ್ಯತರುಣಃ .

ദാശുഷേ ഹവിർദത്തവതേ യജമാനായ ബൃഹദ്വയോ ദധാസി, മഹദന്നം ദീർഘാമായുർവാ പ്രയച്ഛസി . കവിർവിദ്വാൻ . ഗൃഹപതിർഗൃഹസ്യ പാലയിതാ . യുവാ നിത്യതരുണഃ .

Sāyaṇa translation / explanation

Dāśuṣ is the yajamāna who has given the oblation. Bṛhad vayaḥ may mean great food or long life. Agni is wise, guardian of the house, and eternally young.

Mūla translation

O Soma, desire that we live and not die. You delight in praise and are lord of plants. Among the gods Soma is Brahman, among poets the path-knower, among inspired men the ṛṣi, among animals the buffalo, among birds the falcon, among trees the cutting force. Roaring, Soma passes through the purifier.

Sāyaṇa

Mūla translation

Whatever offense, O Varuṇa, we humans commit against the divine people, and whatever of your law we violate through unawareness, do not harm us for that sin. Whatever the ritual players have damaged, whether knowingly or unknowingly, loosen and destroy all those faults, O god. Then, O Varuṇa, may we become dear to you.

Sāyaṇa translation / explanation

The Varuṇa verses encompass conscious and unconscious transgression, including faults introduced by officiants. The prayer asks Varuṇa to loosen and destroy all such faults so that the sacrificers may become dear to him again.
