Sāyaṇa translation / explanation

“O Agni, become āyurdā, a giver of life to the yajamāna.” Agni enjoys the oblation. He is ghṛtapratīka because ghee stands at the beginning of the rite in offerings such as the ādhāra and prayājas. He is ghṛtayoni because ghee also sustains the flame in later offerings such as the anūyājas.

Having drunk the sweet, pleasing, purified ghee of the cow, Agni is asked to protect the yajamāna on every side just as a father protects his son.

On the Ekāṣṭakā, the ordainer of the days, having cooked a cake measuring four śarāvas, one should burn a thicket with it in the morning. Sāyaṇa translation / explanation

Ekāṣṭakā is the eighth day of the dark fortnight of Māgha. It is called the ordainer of the days and elsewhere the wife of the year.

If the fire associated with the cake consumes the entire thicket, the intended undertaking is judged auspicious. If it does not, the undertaking is inauspicious.

Sāyaṇa says that ancient ṛṣis used this as a test before beginning major rites such as year-long sattras.
