Taittirīya Saṃhitā 3.3.10

Dense study edition | animal-sacrifice expiation | pregnant victim | separation of fetus

अनुवाकप्रवेशः

Saṃhitā

—

Sāyaṇa

नवमे जीर्णवृषभालम्भ उक्तोऽनुवाकके ।
अथ दशमे पशुप्रायश्चित्तविशेष उच्यते ।

Sāyaṇa translation / explanation

The ninth anuvāka described the taking of the aged bull. The tenth now teaches a special expiation connected with the animal sacrifice.

यदि पशुर्गर्भिणी ज्ञायेत

Saṃhitā

सूऱ्यो॑ दे॒वो दि॑वि॒षद्भ्यो॑ धा॒ता क्ष॒त्राय॑ वा॒युः प्र॒जाभ्यः॑ ।
बृह॒स्पति॑स्त्वा प्र॒जाप॑तये॒ ज्योति॑ष्मतीं जुहोतु ॥

Mūla translation

May Sūrya, the god, offer you for those who dwell in heaven; Dhātṛ for kṣatra; Vāyu for the creatures; Bṛhaspati for Prajāpati, you who are radiant.

Sāyaṇa

यदि अतिरात्रादिषु पशुविकृतिभूतायां छाग्यां संज्ञप्तायामियं गर्भिणीति बुध्येत, तदा द्वे आहुती जुहुयात् ।

सूर्यो देव इति । सूर्यो देवो दिविषदामभिवृद्ध्यर्थम्, धाता देवः क्षत्त्रावृद्ध्यर्थम्, वायुः प्रजाभिवृद्ध्यर्थम्, बृहस्पतिः प्रजापतिप्राप्त्यर्थं ज्योतिष्मतीं त्वां जुहोतु ।

Sāyaṇa translation / explanation

If, after the female victim has already been killed in an Atirātra or another animal-sacrifice variant, it is discovered that she was pregnant, a special expiation is required.

Sūrya is invoked for the increase of the heavenly beings, Dhātṛ for the increase of kṣatra, Vāyu for the increase of creatures, and Bṛhaspati for connection with Prajāpati. The victim is addressed as jyotiṣmatī, radiant.

यस्यास्ते हरितो गर्भः

Saṃhitā

यस्या᳚स्ते॒ हरि॑तो॒ गर्भोऽथो॒ योनि॑र्हिर॒ण्ययी᳚ ।
अङ्गा॒न्यह्रु॑ता॒ यस्यै॒ तां दे॒वैः सम॑जीगमम् ॥

Mūla translation

She whose embryo is green-golden and whose womb is golden, whose limbs are not injured, her I unite with the gods.

Sāyaṇa

यस्यास्ते हरितो हृतसारो हरितवर्णो वा गर्भः, योनिर्हिरण्मयी हृतसारा हरितवर्णा वा, यस्या अङ्गान्यह्रुता अपिहिता कुटिलानि, तामिमां देवैः संगतां करोमि ।

Sāyaṇa translation / explanation

Sāyaṇa allows harita to suggest either a green-golden color or a condition in which vital essence has been drawn away. The womb is similarly described as hiraṇmayī.

The mantra ritually reconnects the animal with the gods despite the abnormal circumstance created by the hidden pregnancy.

आ वर्तन वर्तय

Saṃhitā

आ व॑र्तन वर्तय॒ नि नि॑वर्तन वर्त॒येन्द्र॑ नर्दबुद ।
भूम्या॒श्चत॑स्रः प्र॒दिश॒स्ताभि॒रा व॑र्तया॒ पुनः॑ ॥

Mūla translation

O turner, turn the fetus. O returner, cause it to come forth. O Indra Nardabuda, with the four quarters of the earth turn it again.

Sāyaṇa

वर्तन, गर्भस्य प्रवर्तक हे देव, गर्भमावर्तय । हे निवर्तन, गर्भस्य निर्गमकृद्देव, गर्भं निवर्तय, निर्गतं कुरु ।

हे इन्द्र, नर्दबुद, गर्भस्य शब्दयितः, भूम्याश्चतस्रः प्रकृष्टा दिशस्ताभिः पुनर्गर्भमावर्तय ।

Sāyaṇa translation / explanation

Vartana is the deity who turns or sets the fetus in motion. Nivartana is the deity who causes it to come out.

Indra is addressed as Nardabuda, associated by Sāyaṇa with sounding or causing sound in relation to the fetus. With the four directions of the earth the fetus is ritually turned again.

वि ते भिनद्मि

Saṃhitā

वि ते॑ भिनद्मि तिक॒रीं वि योनिं॒ वि ग॑वी॒न्यौ᳚ ।
वि मा॒तरं॑ च पु॒त्रं च॒ वि गर्भं॑ च ज॒रायु॑ च ॥

Mūla translation

I separate your enclosing membrane, I separate the womb, I separate the two channels. I separate mother and offspring, fetus and placenta.

Sāyaṇa

हे वशे, ते तव तिकरीं विभिनद्मि, विच्छिनद्मि । योनिं च विभिनद्मि । गवीन्यौ, योन्यन्तर्गतिविशेषनिमित्तभूतौ प्रवर्तकौ, विभिनद्मि ।

मातरं पुत्रं गर्भं जरायु च विभिनद्मि, विश्लेषयामि ।

Sāyaṇa translation / explanation

The mantra accompanies the physical and ritual separation of the fetus from the mother.

Sāyaṇa glosses the listed parts as membranes, womb, and structures involved in passage through the birth channel. Mother, offspring, fetus, and placenta are all ritually separated.

बहिस्ते अस्तु

Saṃhitā

ब॒हिस्ते॑ अस्तु॒ बालिति॑ ॥

Mūla translation

May the vital principle be outside you.

Sāyaṇa

बाल्वालहेतुप्राणवृत्तिरूप आत्मा बहिः सर्वं व्याप्यास्तु, इति विभिनद्मीति पूर्वत्र हेतुः ।

Sāyaṇa translation / explanation

Sāyaṇa takes this as referring to the vital principle or life-function that causes bodily activity. It is to be outside and pervasive. This supplies the rationale for the separation described in the preceding mantra.

उरुद्रप्सो विश्वरूपः

Saṃhitā

उ॒रु॒द्र॒प्सो वि॒श्वरू॑प॒ इन्दुः॒ पव॑मानो॒ धीर॑ आनञ्ज॒ गर्भ᳚म् ॥

Mūla translation

The broad-dropping, all-formed, purified, steady Indu has reached the fetus.

Sāyaṇa

उरुद्रप्सो बहुसारावयवो, विश्वरूपो नानारूपः, इन्दुः शुक्रपवमानः शुद्धरूपो, धीरः स्थिरः ।

ईदृशो यः प्रजापशुरूपोऽवयवसंघातः आनञ्ज, गच्छतु गर्भं जननान्तरम् ।

Sāyaṇa translation / explanation

The fetus is described in Soma-like language: rich in fluid essence, manifold in form, purified, and stable.

Sāyaṇa understands the organized living aggregate as moving toward another birth or continuation of generation.

एकपदी द्विपदी

Saṃhitā

एक॑पदी द्वि॒पदी᳚ त्रि॒पदी॒ चतु॑ष्पदी॒ पञ्च॑पदी॒ षट्प॑दी ।
स॒प्तप॑द्य॒ष्टाप॑दी॒ भुव॒नानु॑ प्रथता॒ग्॒ स्वाहा᳚ ॥

Mūla translation

One-footed, two-footed, three-footed, four-footed, five-footed, six-footed, seven-footed, eight-footed, may she spread through the worlds. Svāhā.

Sāyaṇa

एषा वशा एकपद्यादिरूपा भुवनानि भूतजातान्यनुक्रमेण प्रथताम् ।

Sāyaṇa translation / explanation

The animal is praised through progressive numerical expansion. In these forms she is to extend through the worlds and through the classes of beings.

मही द्यौः पृथिवी च

Saṃhitā

म॒ही द्यौः पृ॑थि॒वी च॑ न इ॒मं य॒ज्ञं मि॑मिक्षताम् ।
पि॒पृ॒तां नो॒ भरी॑मभिः ॥

Mūla translation

May great Heaven and Earth moisten this sacrifice for us. May they sustain us with their forms of support.

Sāyaṇa

महती द्यौः पृथिवी चेमं नो यज्ञं मिमिक्षताम्, आसिञ्चताम् । नोऽस्मांश्च भरीमभिः, भरणप्रकारैः, पिपृतां पालयताम् ।

Sāyaṇa translation / explanation

Heaven and Earth are asked to moisten or anoint the sacrifice and to sustain the sacrificers by their many forms of support.

Ritual: Purpose

This sequence is a prāyaścitta for discovering pregnancy in the sacrificial animal after killing. The rite separates mother, fetus, placenta, and generative structures, then ritually reintegrates the event into cosmic order.
