Taittirīya Saṃhitā 3.3.3

Adābhya and Aṃśu grahas · secret water-names · prāṇa

त्रयः सोमांशवः

Saṃhitā

वस॑वस्त्वा॒ प्र वृ॑हन्तु गाय॒त्रेण॒ छंद॑सा॒ग्नेः प्रि॒यं पाथ॒ उपे॑हि । रु॒द्रास्त्वा॒ प्र वृ॑हन्तु॒ त्रैष्टु॑भेन॒ छंद॒सेन्द्र॑स्य प्रि॒यं पाथ॒ उपे॑हि । आदि॒त्यास्त्वा॒ प्र वृ॑हन्तु॒ जाग॑तेन॒ छंद॑सा॒ विश्वे॑षां दे॒वानां᳚ प्रि॒यं पाथ॒ उपे॑हि ।

Mūla translation

May the Vasus draw you forth with the Gāyatrī metre; go to Agni's dear portion. May the Rudras draw you forth with the Triṣṭubh; go to Indra's dear portion. May the Ādityas draw you forth with the Jagatī; go to the dear portion of all the gods.

Sāyaṇa

हे सोमांशो वसुनामका देवास्त्वां प्रवृहन्तु ... गायत्रेण छन्दसा । तवाऽऽकर्षणे छन्दः साधनम् । तथाविधस्त्वमग्नेः प्रियं पाथोऽन्नभावमुपेहि । एवं द्वितीयतृतीयमन्त्रौ योज्यौ ।

Sāyaṇa translation / explanation

Sāyaṇa treats the metre itself as the ritual means by which each Soma stalk is drawn out. The second and third mantras are understood on the same pattern.

Structure: Threefold mapping

Vasus–Gāyatrī–Agni; Rudras–Triṣṭubh–Indra; Ādityas–Jagatī–Viśve Devas.

अपां गोप्यानि नामानि

Saṃhitā

मान्दा॑सु ते शुक्र शु॒क्रमा धू॑नोमि भ॒न्दना॑सु॒ कोत॑नासु॒ नूत॑नासु॒ रेशी॑षु॒ मेषी॑षु॒ वाशी॑षु विश्व॒भृत्सु॒ माध्वी॑षु ककु॒हासु॒ शक्व॑रीषु शु॒क्रासु॑ ते शुक्र शु॒क्रमा धू॑नोमि ।

Mūla translation

O shining Soma, I agitate your shining essence in the Māndā waters ... in the Bhandanā, Kotanā, Nūtanā, Reśī, Meṣī, Vāśī, Viśvabhṛt, Mādhvī, Kakuhā, Śakvarī, and Śukrā waters.

Sāyaṇa

मान्दास्वित्यादीनि सप्तम्यन्तानि द्वादश पदान्यपां गोप्यानि नामानि । ... मन्दगतय आपो मान्दाः ... विश्वभृत्सु विश्वस्य धारिकासु ... माध्वीषु मधुररसवतीषु ... शक्वरीषु शक्तिमतीषु, शुक्रासु दीप्यमानासु ।

Sāyaṇa translation / explanation

Sāyaṇa calls the twelve expressions secret names of the Waters. He then etymologizes them: slow-moving, auspicious, knowledge-producing, new, swift, striving, sounding, world-supporting, sweet, prominent, powerful, and shining.

Study note: गोप्यानि नामानि

These twelve water-names are explicitly called secret names; this becomes central in 3.3.4.

अदाभ्यग्रहः

Saṃhitā

शु॒क्रं ते॑ शु॒क्रेण॑ गृह्णा॒म्यह्नो॑ रू॒पेण॒ सूर्य॑स्य र॒श्मिभिः॑ । आस्मि॑न्नु॒ग्रा अ॑चुच्यवुर्दि॒वो धारा॑ असश्चत ॥

Mūla translation

Your shining essence I take with a shining essence, with the form of the day and the rays of the Sun. Into this the mighty streams have flowed; the streams from heaven have come together.

Sāyaṇa

हे दधिद्रव्य ते शुक्रं तव सारं शुक्रेण सोमादिरूपेण सारेण सह गृह्णामि । ... अस्मिन्पात्र उग्रा धारास्तीव्राः सोमरसधारा आचुच्यवुरागत्य पतिताः ।

Sāyaṇa translation / explanation

Sāyaṇa interprets śukra as the essence of the curd substance and the Soma-like shining essence with which it is taken. The mighty streams are intense streams of Soma juice entering the vessel.

ककुहं रूपम्

Saṃhitā

क॒कु॒हꣳ रू॒पं वृ॑ष॒भस्य॑ रोचते बृ॒हथ्सोमः॒ सोम॑स्य पुरो॒गाः शु॒क्रः शु॒क्रस्य॑ पुरो॒गाः ।

Mūla translation

The exalted form of the Bull shines forth, great. Soma goes before Soma; the shining one goes before the shining one.

Sāyaṇa

वृषभस्य वर्षयितुरिन्द्रस्य ककुहं ककुद्वत्प्रधानभूतं रूपं वृष्टिलक्षणं स्वरूपं रोचते । ... अयं सोमो वल्लीरूपो राजरूपस्य सोमस्य पुरोगाः ।

Sāyaṇa translation / explanation

The Bull is Indra as rain-giver; his prominent form is rain. Soma as creeper precedes Soma in royal form.

Ritual imagery: वृषभ = इन्द्र

Sāyaṇa explicitly reads the Bull as Indra in his rain-giving aspect.

अदाभ्यनाम

Saṃhitā

यत्ते॑ सो॒मादा᳚भ्यं॒ नाम॒ जागृ॑वि॒ तस्मै॑ ते सोम॒ सोमा॑य॒ स्वाहा ।

Mūla translation

O Soma, that name of yours which is Adābhya, ever wakeful—to that Soma of yours, svāhā.

Sāyaṇa

हे सोम ते यन्नामादाभ्यं केनाप्यतिरस्करणीयं जागृवि सदा जागरणशीलम् ।

Sāyaṇa translation / explanation

Adābhya means incapable of being overpowered or suppressed by anyone; jāgṛvi means ever wakeful.

Lexical: अदाभ्य

Sāyaṇa: केनाप्यतिरस्करणीयम् — not to be overpowered or suppressed by anyone.

प्राणस्य पुनरागमनम्

Saṃhitā

आ नः॑ प्रा॒ण ए॑तु परा॒वत॒ आन्तरि॑क्षाद्दि॒वस्परि॑ । आयुः॑ पृथि॒व्या अध्य॒मृत॑मसि प्रा॒णाय॑ त्वा ।

Mūla translation

May our prāṇa come to us from afar, from the mid-region, from beyond heaven. You are life upon the earth; you are immortality; for prāṇa I take you.

Sāyaṇa

परावतो दूरदेशात्प्राणो नोऽस्मान्प्रत्येतु आगच्छतु । ... हे हिरण्य पृथिव्या अध्युपरि त्वमायुरमृतमसि आयुर्हेतुरमृतत्वहेतुश्चासि ।

Sāyaṇa translation / explanation

The prāṇa is recalled from afar, the mid-region, and beyond heaven. Gold is treated as a cause of life and immortality and is used for restoring prāṇa.

Ritual: Gold and breath

If the prescribed breathlessness is interrupted, breath is restored over gold with this mantra.

अनिरुक्ता प्राजापत्या

Saṃhitā

द॒ध॒न्वे वा॒ यदी॒मनु॒ वोच॒द्ब्रह्मा॑णि॒ वेरु॒ तत् । परि॒ विश्वा॑नि॒ काव्या॑ ने॒मिश्च॒क्रमि॑वाभवत् ॥

Mūla translation

Whatever the Veda has successively declared concerning the sacrificial brahmans, that I retain. It has encompassed all sacred works as the rim encompasses the wheel.

Sāyaṇa

वेदोक्तं किमपि न विस्मरामीत्यर्थः । ... यथा रथस्य चक्रं कृत्स्नं परितो नेमिर्व्याप्नोति तद्वत् । अत्र देवताविशेषस्य कस्यचिदपि विस्पष्टमप्रतीतत्वादियमनिरुक्ता प्राजापत्या ।

Sāyaṇa translation / explanation

Sāyaṇa paraphrases the core idea as 'I forget nothing taught by the Veda.' Because no particular deity is clearly indicated, the mantra is anirukta and assigned to Prajāpati.

Study note: अनिरुक्ता प्राजापत्या

Where no specific deity is clearly indicated, Sāyaṇa assigns the mantra to Prajāpati.
