A critical manual of Sandhyopāsanā and its textual foundations, developed alongside the Taittirīya Saṃhitā study.

important Current scope
This vault contains a verified study starting point and research scaffolding, not an established full Sandhyāvandanam procedure for any tradition. Read procedural placeholders as research tasks, not ritual directions.

Key policy

Source layers remain distinct: (1) Vedic and early Sūtra/Smṛti testimony, (2) exegetical and nibandha synthesis, especially Gopīnātha, (3) documented contemporary procedures and later additions. Later does not itself mean invalid or optional.
