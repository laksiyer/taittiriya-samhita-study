सन्ध्यावन्दनम्

A source-critical manual of Sandhyopāsanā based on the Saṃskāraratnamālā of Gopīnātha Dīkṣita, with comparative Vedic traditions and commentaries.

Editorial principle. Distinguish the early attested injunction, its later śāstric interpretation, and the received ritual procedure. This is a developing study site, not yet a complete performance manual.

सन्ध्योपासनायाः शास्त्रीयाधारः

Opening chapter: the timing and daily obligation of Sandhyā, with Manu 2.101 as the first established textual witness.

मनुस्मृतिः २.१०१

पूर्वां सन्ध्यां जपंस्तिष्ठेत् सावित्रीमाऽर्कदर्शनात् ।
पश्चिमां तु समासीनः सम्यग् ऋक्षविभावनात् ॥

Translation: At the morning twilight, one should stand reciting the Sāvitrī until the sun is seen; at the evening twilight, one should sit until the stars become distinctly visible.

Source: Manusmṛti 2.101. Translational interpretation checked against Ganganath Jha's edition of Medhātithi's commentary. Text and commentary.

विवेचनम् · Initial exegetical note

The verse explicitly concerns the Sāvitrī recitation, posture, and twilight period. It does not by itself furnish the full sequence familiar in current Sandhyāvandanam. Medhātithi discusses whether standing and sitting or the recitation itself is the principal enjoined element, and considers the relationship of these injunctions to Agnihotra. These questions must be read in full before treating a modern sequence as the direct content of Manu's rule.

Jha's comparative notes point to Gautama Dharmasūtra 2.17, Baudhāyana Dharmasūtra 4.2.10–13, Āpastamba Dharmasūtra 1.11.30.8, and Yājñavalkya Smṛti 1.24–25. These are research leads, not yet checked against independent critical editions here.

अध्ययनक्रमः · Planned studies

  1. सन्ध्याकालः, नित्यत्वम्, अधिकारः: times, obligation, and eligibility.
  2. सावित्री तथा जपः: text, Vedic setting, and daily recitation.
  3. आचमनम्, मार्जनम्, प्राशनम्: actions and attested authorities.
  4. अर्घ्यप्रदानम्: water offering, prescribed counts, explanations.
  5. उपस्थानम्: Vedic mantras and tradition-specific variations.
  6. प्रचलितप्रयोगः: documented later additions, Purāṇic and Āgamic elements.

ग्रन्थसूची · Primary and interpretive bibliography

The Saṃskāraratnamālā is our guiding nibandha. Smṛti and Dharmasūtra passages will be verified against source editions rather than inferred from later prayogas.

  1. Gopīnātha Dīkṣita, Saṃskāraratnamālā. Central śāstric framework. Digitized volume 2. Exact Sandhyā section/page pending collation.
  2. Sandhyābhāṣyasamuccaya (Ānandāśrama, 1899). Five commentaries, identities and exact readings to be catalogued. Edition listing.
  3. B. V. Kamesvara Aiyar, The Sandhyavandanam of Rig, Yajus, and Sâma Vedins (1898). Comparative translation and interpretation. Digitized book.
  4. Vedāntī Sītārāma Śāstrī, Kṛṣṇayajurvedīya Sandhyāvandanam sabhāṣyam (1931). Witness to received procedure and commentary. Scan.
  5. Manusmṛti 2.101, with Medhātithi. First worked example of textual exegesis. Source.
  6. Gautama, Baudhāyana, Āpastamba, Hiraṇyakeśin and other Kalpa/Dharma works. Exact passages and edition-level readings to be added as verified.
