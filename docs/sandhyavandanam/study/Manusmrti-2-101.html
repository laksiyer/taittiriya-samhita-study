Translation

At the morning twilight, one should stand reciting the Sāvitrī until the sun is seen. At the evening twilight, one should sit until the stars become distinctly visible.

विवेचनम्vivecanamvivechanam𑌵𑌿𑌵𑍇𑌚𑌨𑌮𑍍వివేచనంವಿವೇಚನಂവിവേചനം

This passage concerns posture, recitation of the Sāvitrī, and the periods of morning and evening twilight. It does not, by itself, prescribe the entire later Sandhyāvandanam sequence.

In the translation of Medhātithi's commentary associated with Ganganath Jha, discussion includes the relation of posture to recitation and other obligations. Read the whole commentary in context before extracting a prescriptive conclusion.
