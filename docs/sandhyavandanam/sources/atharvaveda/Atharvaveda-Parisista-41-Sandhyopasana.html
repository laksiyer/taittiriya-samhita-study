English translation

Having thoroughly washed the hands, feet and face, and having sat facing east in vīrāsana, one should sip water with the mantra beginning “Jīvā stha,” sprinkle oneself with “Āpo hi ṣṭhā,” perform prāṇāyāmas, sip water again, and rise. Holding water in the right hand, one should release the water in several streams with the mantra beginning “Ayojālā.”

The ten directions are her flanks; all branches of knowledge and grammar [are associated with] her belly; Earth is her hips; Wind her station; the stars her ornaments.
